Capture

Time, mileage, expenses, receipt photos, invoices and estimates, with or without a connection. They wait on this device and are entered in OpenBook when it's back online, by the same rules as the online forms.

Open OpenBook

The photo goes to the receipts inbox, where OpenBook reads the vendor, date and total for you to record.

Lines

OpenBook numbers it, prices lines left without a rate ("Auto") for the customer, and adds the customer's sales tax when it's sent. An invoice is posted then; an estimate posts nothing.

On this device

    Drafts are kept encrypted on this device until they're entered. Entered and dismissed drafts are removed from it; ones that need attention are listed in OpenBook under Capture, and leave here once they're dealt with there.
