# Code of conduct

OpenBook is made by people with different skills, backgrounds and ways of working: bookkeepers, accountants, developers, people who use assistive technology. Everyone is welcome to take part.

## What we ask

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new to the code, to bookkeeping or to English.
- Talk about the work, not the person. Disagree with an idea by explaining why, and accept that the answer may still be no.
- Assume good intent, and ask before assuming the worst.
- Keep other people's private information private, including anything from their books.

## What isn't accepted

Harassment, insults, threats, sexual remarks or attention, discrimination of any kind, deliberately disrupting discussions, and publishing someone's private details. This applies in issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews and anywhere else people meet around the project.

## If something goes wrong

Tell the maintainers privately: on GitHub, open the repository's **Security** tab, choose **Report a vulnerability** and say it's about conduct, or contact a maintainer through their GitHub profile. Reports are kept confidential. The maintainers may remove comments, lock conversations, or block someone from the project, as they judge fair.
