# Sales and receivables

Developer notes moved out of `CLAUDE.md` (index there). Read this file before changing this area; keep it up to date in the same commit.

## Sales (M3)

- `Customer` and `Item` (products and services; `Service` or `NonInventory`, each with a default price and income account) are deactivated, never deleted. A service or non-inventory item may post to an other current liability instead (`Item.EnsureIncomeAccount(account, type)`): gift cards, sold as a line and redeemed by a negative one; the item form lists those liabilities except the sales tax agencies' and their parent. `Item.Category` (≤ 50, `ItemDetails.Category` null keeps it, blank clears) feeds report `sales-by-category` (`ActivityReports.SalesByCategoryAsync`, the sales-by-item figures grouped). Names are unique ignoring case. Customers with unpaid invoices can't be deactivated. `Customer.CreditLimit` (nullable, column `CreditLimitMinor`) and `CreditHold` only warn: `CustomerService.CreditWarningAsync` (the billed-to customer's balance with everything billed with it) on the new invoice, sales order and estimate forms.
- `Invoice`, `CustomerPayment` and `SalesReceipt` are documents posting one journal entry each (`Source = sales.*`), voided never edited, except invoices: `InvoiceService.ChangeAsync` corrects one in place (same id and number) by voiding its entry (reversal on the original date) and posting a new one, with its stock reversed and planned again, in one save; so an invoice has one standing entry (`JournalEntryId`) and may have voided ones with the same `SourceId`. The customer can't change, the total can't go below `AmountPaid`, lines from a sales order or estimate (`SourceLines`) and billable expense/time/mileage lines (by `PriceSource`) must be passed back unchanged, and when the income per account and class changes, the dates payments and retainers were applied must be open too (cash basis allocates over current lines). Lines are renumbered in their new order; `ChangeInvoiceCommand.FromLines` says which old line each continues, and `CustomFieldStamper.MoveLinesAsync` moves their custom field values. The `Updated` audit record's after-snapshot carries `ChangeReason` (`ChangesAsync` reads them for the invoice page). Page `/sales/invoices/{id}/edit`, API `PUT /api/sales/invoices/{id}`. `SalesReceiptService.ChangeAsync` does the same for a receipt (customer can change, or be none; deposit account too; while in a bank deposit the total and deposit account must stay, `receipt.deposited`; reconciled ones are refused by `StageVoidAsync`). Page `/sales/receipts/{id}/edit`, API `PUT /api/sales/receipts/{id}`. Lines (`SalesLine`) need an item when they have an amount; item-less lines are notes. Rates and quantities allow 4 decimals (stored as ten-thousandths); line amounts round half away from zero to the cent. `SalesLine.Postings` credits each income account with its lines' sum (debits it if a discount makes it negative).
- Invoices post to the first active A/R account; one is created and saved on first use if none exists. Payments deposit to a Bank or Other current asset account (Undeposited Funds by default when the chart has it). Invoice `AmountPaid` is everything settled (payments, early payment discounts, applied credit) and is kept in step by payment create/void/change and credit application. `PaymentService.ChangeAsync` corrects a payment in place (entry voided and reposted; applications replaced, each invoice's old settlement removed and the new one applied, invoices audited; customer and receivable account fixed; credit applied later keeps its `AppliedOn` unless before the new date; unapplied ≥ refunded). The amount and deposit account stay while it's deposited, matched to a feed line (the match follows) or taken online (a `PaymentLink.CustomerPaymentId` points at it; the reference stays too, since Stripe payouts clear by it). Page `/sales/payments/{id}/edit`, API `PUT /api/sales/payments/{id}`. An invoice with anything applied can't be voided.
- Returned payments (bounced checks, 2026-11-07): `PaymentService.ReturnAsync(id, date, fee, feeAccountId, reason)` posts `CustomerPayment.ReturnSource` (`sales.payment_return`, `SourceId` = the payment): Dr A/R amount + discount, Cr the bank (the deposit's, or the payment's own bank account; refused while in Undeposited Funds, `payment.not_in_bank`), discount lines reversed, an optional fee Dr "Bank Service Charges" (`SalesAccounts.GetOrCreateBankChargesAsync`) / Cr bank. The payment stays posted with `ReturnedOn`, `ReturnEntryId`, `ReturnAccountId`, `ReturnFee`, `ReturnReason`; its applications stay, `StandsOn(date)` says whether they settle anything then (aging, statements: a return line), `Unapplied` is 0, and invoices' `AmountPaid` drops. Change, void, refund, apply credit and the deposit's change or void are refused (`payment.returned`, `deposit.payment_returned`); `UndoReturnAsync` voids the return entry unless an invoice was paid another way since. Cash basis: `ReturnSource` is an accrual document; each return in range keeps its non-A/R lines, takes the invoices' income back through a negative `SettlementPlan` turn (source = the return entry) and puts the unapplied part back in A/R. The books check counts it in `ReceivableSources` and as the payment's second document. Page: the payment's "The payment came back"; API `POST /api/sales/payments/{id}/return[/undo]`. Not done: returned gifts, chargebacks on channel payouts.
- Document services check everything before `StageAsync` and mutate documents/invoices only after staging succeeds.
- Customer and item CSV imports (`SalesImports.cs`) follow the chart import pattern (preview, all-or-nothing, existing names skipped). `CsvColumns` is the shared header matcher.
- SQLite can't `ORDER BY` a `DateTimeOffset`; order such results in memory.
- `Web/Components/Links.cs` maps a journal entry's source to its document page; use it wherever a ledger line links somewhere.

## Receivables (V1.3)

- Sales lines have a `SalesLineKind`: Item, Note, DiscountPercent (percent of the item lines above it) or DiscountAmount. Discount lines post to the income account "Discounts Given" (`SalesAccounts.GetOrCreateDiscountsAsync`). Build lines with `SalesLineBuilder`, never `SalesLine.FromDrafts` directly in services.
- Terms are `PaymentTerms` (due days plus optional early payment discount) from the customer, copied onto each invoice (`EarlyPaymentDiscountPercent`/`Date`). A payment application has `Amount` (money) and `Discount` (posted Dr Discounts Given); `Settled` is both.
- A `CustomerPayment` has an explicit `Amount`; what isn't applied is customer credit (`Unapplied`). Credit (credit memo balance or unapplied payment) is applied to invoices by `CreditService.ApplyAsync`, which posts nothing; applications carry `AppliedOn` dates so aging and statements work as of a past date. Applying more of the same source to the same invoice adds to the existing application (keeps the first date). `ApplyAsync` needs the date open (`EnsureOpen`), and on a company whose default basis is cash the payment's date too, since cash basis counts it there; not on accrual, or a prepayment could never be used once its year is closed. `CreditService.UnapplyAsync` and `VendorCreditService.UnapplyAsync` take a credit memo or vendor credit back off an invoice or bill (the date applied must be open; audited on both; `POST /sales/credit-memos/{id}/applications/{invoiceId}/remove`, `/purchases/vendor-credits/{id}/applications/{billId}/remove`, API `DELETE` on `/applications/{id}`). Payment credit comes off through `PaymentService.ChangeAsync`.
- `CreditMemo` posts the mirror of an invoice (Dr income, Cr A/R) and shares the invoice number sequence (each refuses a number the other uses; `SalesAccounts.NextNumber` increments the trailing digits of the highest, keeping prefix and padding). `CreditService.ChangeCreditMemoAsync` corrects one in place like an invoice (customer fixed; total ≥ applied + refunded; date no later than the first application; when the income per account and class changes, refund dates must be open, since cash basis allocates refunds over the current lines; returned stock reversed and planned again). Page `/sales/credit-memos/{id}/edit`, API `PUT /api/sales/credit-memos/{id}`. `CustomerRefund` pays credit back (Dr A/R, Cr bank/card) from one credit memo or one payment.
- Invoices, estimates, credit memos and sales receipts keep `BillToAddress`/`ShipToAddress` (`KeepAddresses`, set at creation by `SalesAddresses.KeepAsync`: the billed customer's billing address, the job's else the billed customer's shipping address; corrections and estimate edits keep them); `FormService` prints them, falling back to today's customer for older documents (null).
- `Estimate` posts nothing; changing the customer, lines or total of one answered online clears the answer and puts it back to Pending; `InvoiceService` closes it when invoiced (`CreateInvoiceCommand.EstimateId`) and reopens it when that invoice is voided.
- Payment methods (2026-11-03): `PaymentMethod` (Cash, Check, Card, BankTransfer, Other; `PaymentMethods.Label`) on `CustomerPayment`, `SalesReceipt`, `Donation` and `Retainer` (`SetMethod`, nullable, set after `Create`/`Change`; commands take `Method`, change commands keep it when null; online payments are Card). `UndepositedItem.Method` feeds the deposit form's "Tick only" buttons and `ListReports.DepositSlipAsync` (cash/check/other totals; `GET /banking/deposits/{id}/slip` and `/api/sales/deposits/{id}/slip` as PDF through `IReportExporter`). `PaymentMethodField` (Components/Sales) on the interactive forms.
- Undeposited funds: payments, receipts, retainers and gifts in an Other current asset account are grouped by `DepositService` into a `BankDeposit` (`Source = bank.deposit`: Dr bank, Cr holding account). Deposited items can't be voided until the deposit is. `BankDeposit.Extras` (owned `BankDepositExtras`, `DepositExtraDraft`, at most `MaxExtras`; `MakeDepositCommand.Extras`): signed lines besides the items, negative = a fee or cash back (Dr its account), positive = other money (Cr its account), in `Total` and the bank debit; not A/R, A/P or the deposit's own bank; the total must stay above zero (`deposit.total_not_positive`); on the slip, the detail page and the API. Kinds outside Sales register an `IUndepositedSource` (marked and cleared in the deposit's save; `DescribeAsync` gives deposited ones' dates and labels for changing a deposit); `Links.ForDeposited` gives each kind's page.
- Cash basis (`ReportLedger`): credit memos and refunds are accrual documents. Payments recognize applied amounts plus discounts against the invoice lines (credit applied later counts on the payment's date), keep the unapplied part in A/R, and include the discount lines as posted. A credit memo refund reverses income on the refund date; a payment refund counts as posted.
- Money ahead of its document: an application to an invoice or bill dated after the as-of date isn't a settlement of anything then, so the agings count it as credit (`ReceivableDocuments.InvoicesDatedLater`, `VendorAging.PaidAhead`), and they keep tying to A/R and A/P. Refunds can't be dated before their source, nor a source moved past its refund (`refund.before_source`). `CustomerSummary`/`VendorSummary.Balance` is open documents less unused credit, as on the aging; `CustomerService.StatementListAsync` rolls billed sub-customers into their parent, once each. A sub-customer settled by another customer's money stays billed with it (`customer.settled_by_parent`).
- `ReceivablesService` computes aging and statements from the documents, not the ledger. `ReceivablesService.Aging(customers, documents, asOf)` is the calculation; `AgingAsync` feeds it `ISalesStore.ListReceivablesAsync(asOf)`: invoices dated by then that are unpaid now or settled by something dated after it, whatever settled them, and payments and credit memos holding credit now or used or refunded after the date, with their refunds (exactly the documents that can matter; `BooksCheckTests`' random runs compare it with every document). Statements and one customer's aging use `ListReceivablesForAsync(customerIds)` (those customers' documents plus anything others applied to their invoices). Settlements are found from the applications (indexed by invoice) to their documents, selecting the owned rows' foreign keys (`EF.Property<Guid>(a, "CustomerPaymentId")`): selecting the owner's id inside the collection needs an APPLY, which SQLite can't do. Forms: `FormService` builds `SalesForm`/`StatementForm`, `IFormRenderer` (MigraDoc) renders them. PDFsharp-MigraDoc is pinned to `[6.2.4]`, the last MIT release (6.5+ is AGPL or commercial): never let it be upgraded. Its Core build needs `SystemFontResolver`, which maps the one family forms use ("OpenBook Sans") to a system sans-serif, or to OpenBookSans-Regular/Bold.ttf in a `fonts` folder beside the app. PDFs are served inline at `/api/sales/{kind}/{id}/pdf`.
- Email: `EmailService` with `IEmailSender` (MailKit; "folder" delivery writes .eml files to `mail/` beside the company file). The SMTP password is encrypted by the host's `ISecretProtector` (Data Protection; tests use a marker). Every send writes an `AuditAction.Emailed` record. `RecurringPostingService` also creates (and emails) automatic recurring invoices. Recurring templates (invoice, bill, expense, gift) make each occurrence and advance in one `IUnitOfWork` transaction, so a failure between can't repeat it; a refusal discards the scope's tracked changes, so each template is loaded afresh.
- Scheduled invoice emails: `ScheduledEmail` (Domain/Sales; audit `scheduled_email`, linked to `/sales/scheduled-emails`): the message fixed when scheduled, `SendAt` in UTC (later than now, within a year), Scheduled → Sent / Failed (reason kept; `RetryAsync` puts it back to go on the next run) / Cancelled; never deleted. `ScheduledEmailService.SendDueAsync` sends each due one through `EmailService.SendFormAsync` (so the PDF shows the invoice as it is then) and saves each on its own; an invoice voided, paid or written off since is cancelled, not sent (decided when due, so a payment taken back leaves it to go; one already settled can't be scheduled, `scheduled_email.invoice_settled`). `ScheduledEmailHostedService` runs it every minute as "system". The invoice email form (`/sales/email/invoices/{id}`) has "Send later" with a `datetime-local` in the company's time zone; the invoice page and `/sales/scheduled-emails` list them with cancel / try again (posts in `CollectionEndpoints`). API `/api/sales/invoices/{id}/scheduled-emails` (GET, POST with `SendAt` and optional to/subject/body, defaulting to the usual draft), `/api/sales/scheduled-emails` and `/{id}/cancel|retry`.
- New audit entity types: estimate, credit_memo, refund, deposit, recurring_invoice, email_settings, form_style (a form design; all in `Links.ForAuditEntity`).

## Collections (L3, slice 1)

- Sub-customers: `Customer.ParentId` (rules in `CustomerTree`: max 5 levels, no cycles, full names "Parent:Child", `Ordered` for tree order; names stay unique across all customers). `CustomerService.CheckParentAsync` refuses inactive new parents, cycles and too-deep moves (counting the levels below the customer); a parent with active sub-customers can't be deactivated, nor a sub-customer reactivated under an inactive parent. `BillWithParent`: `CustomerTree.BillTo` walks up to who's billed, and invoice, estimate and credit memo forms and emails are addressed there, with a "For" fact naming the project or job; the parent's payments and credits can settle billed sub-customers' invoices (`PaymentService.BilledAsync`, used by payments and `CreditService.ApplyAsync`), its statement includes them (a billed sub-customer's statement is the parent's), and reminders go to the parent. A/R aging still lists each customer's own invoices, in tree order; settlements count from any payer.
- Bad debt: `BadDebtWriteOff` (`Source = sales.write_off`, audit `write_off`, attachments allowed) per invoice: Dr "Bad Debts" (expense, created on first use) or a chosen expense/other expense account, Cr the invoice's A/R; settles the invoice through `AmountPaid`, so the invoice can't be voided while it stands. `WriteOffService.WriteOffAsync` takes several invoices in one save (one entry each); void puts the balance back. Aging and statements count write-offs by date. Cash basis drops them entirely (in `AccrualDocuments` with no derivation: the unpaid income was never recognized). Sales tax on written-off invoices stays owed.
- `CollectionSettings` (one row, everything off until saved; audit `collection_settings` on the company): late fees (percent of the overdue balance to 4 decimals + flat amount, grace days, repeat every N days or once, automatic or not) and reminders (schedule of days from the due date, -90..365, at most 6; subject/body templates with `{customer} {invoice} {balance} {due} {days} {when} {company}`, filled by `CollectionSettings.Fill`).
- Overlapping runs: a fee records `Invoice.LateFeeOn`, unique with `LateFeeForInvoiceId` (null on older fees), and a clash counts as already charged; a reminder's row is saved before the email goes (removed if it fails), so the second run stops on the unique index.
- An invoice with a standing late fee can't be voided (`invoice.has_late_fees`). Automatic recurring invoices that fail to email wait as failed `ScheduledEmail`s, on the to-do list; a recurring template that stops fails its job step, so the System page shows it.
- Late fees: `LateFeeService.DueAsync(today)` lists open invoices (not fees themselves) past `due + grace` whose last fee (voided ones count, since voiding waives it) is older than the repeat period; customers with `NoLateFees` or inactive are skipped. `ChargeAsync` makes one invoice per fee through `InvoiceService` (internal `lateFeeFor` sets `Invoice.LateFeeForInvoiceId`), due on receipt, untaxed, for the "Late Fee" service item (income "Late Fee Income", other income; both created on the first charge and remembered on the settings).
- Reminders: `InvoiceReminder` rows (unique per invoice and step). `ReminderService.DueAsync` picks, per open invoice, the latest step reached that's on or after the invoice date and later than any sent, so a missed step is skipped rather than sent late; `SendAsync` emails the invoice PDF through `EmailService.SendFormAsync`, records each send on its own and keeps going after a failure (stops on email not configured).
- `RecurringPostingService` also charges late fees and sends reminders hourly when they're set to be automatic. Pages `/sales/collections` (what's due today, with ticked rows), `/sales/collections/settings` (accountant), `/sales/write-offs/new` (overdue by N days, or `?invoice=`), `/sales/write-offs/{id}`; customer pages add the parent picker, "bill with parent", no reminders, no late fees and a sub-customer list. Form posts in `Web/Endpoints/CollectionEndpoints.cs`; API under `/api/sales` (`/write-offs`, `/collections/settings`, `/late-fees`, `/reminders`).

## Sales flow (L3, slice 2)

- The invoice that clears a line charges what's left of its amount (`PartialInvoicing.Billed`, worked out from quantities and the line's rate), so progress invoices add up to the source.
- Partial invoicing (`Domain/Sales/SalesOrder.cs`, `PartialInvoicing`): an invoice made from a sales order or estimate records `Invoice.SourceLines` (owned `InvoiceSourceLines`: source line number + quantity) and `SalesOrderId` (estimates use the existing `EstimateId`). What's invoiced is summed from the source's posted invoices, so voiding an invoice frees its quantities with nothing to undo. Item lines bill by quantity (`ByPercent` scales every item line, 4 decimals, capped at what's left); an amount discount goes whole on the first invoice that bills an item (tracked as quantity 1); percent discounts and notes go on every invoice; tax is recalculated each time. Lines keep the source's price (`ToDraft`, no repricing); lots are kept only when the whole line is billed. `InvoiceService.CreateAsync(..., source: InvoiceSource)` closes the order/estimate when `Completes`; voiding reopens a closed one.
- `SalesOrder` (audit `sales_order`, attachments allowed): posts nothing, holds no stock, own number sequence, customer PO number (copied into the invoice memo as "Your PO …"), warehouse. Open → Closed (all invoiced) or Cancelled (`CancelAsync` stops the rest). Editable only until the first invoice. `SalesOrderService.InvoiceAsync(id, InvoiceFromSourceCommand)`: chosen `Lines`, a `Percent`, or (neither) everything left.
- An invoice from an estimate prints the contract position as facts (`FormService.InvoiceAsync`: Contract, Billed before, Left after this; earlier posted invoices of the estimate by date then creation). No retainage.
- Progress invoicing: `EstimateService.ProgressInvoiceAsync` (same command); `ConvertToInvoiceAsync` now invoices what's left. An estimate can't be edited after its first progress invoice (`estimate.invoiced`) and closes when fully invoiced. Old estimates invoiced whole have invoices without source lines; they're already closed.
- Retainers (`Retainer`, audit `retainer`, attachments allowed): received into a Bank account or Undeposited Funds (then Make deposit), Dr bank / Cr "Customer Deposits" (other current liability, created on first use), `Source = sales.retainer`. Applications (`sales.retainer_application`, owned `RetainerApplications`, own entry Dr deposits / Cr A/R, settle the invoice via `AmountPaid`; the retainer's customer or a sub-customer billed with it) and refunds (`sales.retainer_refund`, owned `RetainerRefunds`, Dr deposits / Cr bank or card) can each be voided (reversal entry, `IsVoided`); `SourceId` is the retainer for all three. Aging and statements count applications by `AppliedOn`. Cash basis: receipts and refunds as posted; applications are in `AccrualDocuments` and derived as Dr deposits / Cr the invoice's income lines (like a payment). Report `retainer-balances` (`ListReports.RetainerBalancesAsync`, as of a date); statements add a sentence with what's held on their end date.
- Billable expenses (`BillableExpense`, audit `billable_expense`): a category line of a posted bill (`BillLine.LineNumber`, no item, cost > 0) or expense (a debit line of the spend's journal entry other than the bank), marked after posting with a customer and markup (`BillableExpenseService.MarkAsync`; unique per document and line; removable until invoiced, audited `Deleted`). `CreateInvoiceCommand.BillableExpenseIds` adds them as "Billable Expenses" lines (service item, income "Billable Expense Income", untaxed, price source "Billable expense"; created on first use) and sets `InvoiceId`; voiding the invoice clears it. `BillService.VoidAsync` / `BankingService.VoidAsync` call `BillableExpenseService.ReleaseAsync` first: refused if a cost is invoiced, else the marks are removed.
- Pages: `/sales/orders`, `/sales/orders/new`, `/sales/orders/{id}/edit`, `/sales/orders/{id}`; estimates' "Turn into an invoice" is the shared `PartialInvoiceForm` (Components/Sales; posts `mode` rest/percent/lines, `percent`, `qty:{line}`); `/sales/retainers`, `/sales/retainers/new`, `/sales/retainers/{id}`; `/sales/billable`; `BillableLinesPanel` on bill and expense pages; `RetainerOffer` (Components/Sales) on open invoices lists `RetainerService.AvailableForInvoiceAsync` with an apply button (the apply post takes `back` to an invoice page); the new invoice form lists the customer's open billable expenses to tick. Form posts in `Web/Endpoints/SalesFlowEndpoints.cs`; API under `/api/sales` (`/orders`, `/estimates/{id}/progress(-invoice)`, `/retainers`, `/billable-expenses`). `PriceNote` hides the list price when the item has none (0).

## Getting paid online (L3, slice 4)

- Online payments (`Domain/Sales/OnlinePayments.cs`): `OnlinePaymentSettings` (one row; Stripe only; secret key and webhook signing secret through `ISecretProtector`, never shown, excluded from the export and audit; deposit account (bank or other current asset, usually Undeposited Funds); `PublicUrl` for return pages; `IsReady` = enabled with all three; admin page `/settings/online-payments`, audit `online_payment_settings`). `IPaymentGateway` (Application) with `StripeGateway` (Infrastructure/Payments: Checkout Sessions over REST, form-encoded, Bearer key, amounts in cents; tests use `FakeStripe` behind it, like `FakeSimpleFinBridge`).
- `PaymentLink` per checkout (unique `SessionId`): `OnlinePaymentService.LinkForInvoiceAsync` reuses an open, unexpired link for the same balance, else creates one (success/cancel URLs `/pay/done|cancelled?invoice=`). Paid links record an ordinary `CustomerPayment` (invoice's customer, applied up to the balance, the rest as credit; reference = PaymentIntent id) with the link marked paid in the payment's own save, so repeats are ignored. Three ways in: the webhook `POST /hooks/stripe` (anonymous, `StripeSignature` HMAC check, 5-minute tolerance; `checkout.session.completed`/`async_payment_succeeded` with `payment_status=paid`, and `expired`), `CheckAsync` (button on the invoice's `OnlinePaymentPanel`), and the `/pay/done` page asking about the invoice's open links. Requests without a user act as "online payment" (`OnlinePaymentActor` sets the circuit principal). The emailed "Pay now" button goes through OpenBook when customers can reach it (`/view/{token}/pay`, a `DocumentLink`, made even for an email attaching the PDF): a Stripe session lasts a day, so a fresh one is made at the click, and paid or voided invoices say so. Without a link address it's the session's own URL. Stripe fees aren't recorded. `charge.refunded` / `charge.dispute.created` on a paid link's PaymentIntent become `OnlinePaymentNotice`s (unique `EventId`, posting nothing, audited `Updated` on the invoice; `ResolveNoticeAsync`), shown on the invoice's `OnlinePaymentPanel` and the to-do list; API `GET /api/sales/online-notices`, `POST /{id}/resolve`. `ExpireOutdatedAsync` (every minute, in `ScheduledEmailHostedService`, step "Payment links") expires at Stripe any open link whose amount the invoice no longer owes, however it was settled or changed; nothing that saves an invoice calls Stripe. A link found paid then is recorded as usual, the money landing as credit.
- Customer portal: `PortalAccess` (token `obp_…` shown once, SHA-256 hash stored and excluded from the export, `Hint`, revoked not deleted, last use at most once a minute). `CustomerPortalService`: links per customer (`PortalLinks` on the customer page, created by a form handled in the component; revoke posts to `/customers/{id}/portal/{accessId}/revoke`), `ViewAsync` (open invoices, paid ones of the last year, a 90-day statement; a billed sub-customer sees its parent's account), `InvoiceForAsync` (only invoices of the customer or those billed to it). Pages `/portal/{token}` and `/pay/done|cancelled` are `[AllowAnonymous]` with `PortalLayout` (no app navigation); `/portal/{token}/invoices/{id}/pdf` and `/portal/{token}/invoices/{id}/pay` are anonymous endpoints. `AccessRules` lets any role through `/portal`, `/pay/`, `/hooks`.
- Estimates in the portal (2026-11-03): `PortalView.Estimates` (pending, then answered in the last 90 days); `CustomerPortalService.EstimateForAsync` / `RespondAsync(token, id, accept, name, note)` → `Estimate.Respond` (pending and not expired; a name is required to accept; `RespondedBy/At`, `ResponseNote`), audited on the estimate with actor `portal: {customer}`. `Estimate.SetStatus` (the business's own choice) clears the answer. Anonymous `GET /portal/{token}/estimates/{id}/pdf` and `POST /portal/{token}/estimates/{id}/accept|decline` in `OnlineEndpoints`.
- Receipt capture (`Domain/Purchases/ReceiptCapture.cs`, audit `receipt`): `ReceiptService.UploadAsync` checks the file like an attachment first, saves a `ReceiptCapture`, attaches the file (entity type `receipt`), reads it through `IReceiptTextExtractor` (`ReceiptTextExtractor`: PDF text with PdfPig 0.1.16, Apache-2.0; text files; images only through the Tesseract CLI at `OpenBook:Ocr:TesseractPath`, registered by the host; nothing leaves the server) and fills vendor/date/total/tax with `ReceiptParser` (known vendor names win; last non-subtotal "total" line, else the largest amount). `RecordAsync` posts an expense (one split; category given or the vendor's default) and copies the file onto the bank transaction; `DismissAsync`. Pages `/purchases/receipts` (upload, multipart) and `/purchases/receipts/{id}`; API `/api/purchases/receipts` (raw body + `?fileName=`).
- Form posts and the anonymous endpoints live in `Web/Endpoints/OnlineEndpoints.cs`; API: `/api/sales/invoices/{id}/payment-links`, `/api/sales/payment-links/{id}/check`, `/api/settings/online-payments` (admin).

## Advanced pricing (L12)

- Domain in `Domain/Pricing/Pricing.cs`: `PriceLevel` (a customer's baseline: `FixedPercent` on every item, or `PerItem` prices; `Customer.PriceLevelId`), `PriceRule` (promotions and exceptions: items (none = all), all customers / chosen customers / chosen levels, date range, quantity range, `Percent`/`Amount`/`SetPrice`, priority, exclusive, optional rounding; targets are owned `PriceRuleTarget`s), `QuantityBreak`s owned by `Item` (percent off or unit price from a quantity), `Company.PriceRounding` (default Cent). Levels and rules are deactivated, never deleted; names unique ignoring case.
- `Prices.Quote` is the one place prices are worked out (server and the sales form preview): item rate → level → quantity break (never raises the price) → rules (non-exclusive stack by priority; if any exclusive rule matches, only exclusive rules count and the lowest price wins) → clamp at zero → rounding (the deciding rule's, else the company's), only when something changed the price. Quantity is the item's total across the document's lines (`PricingContext.Quantities`).
- `SalesLineDraft.AutoPrice` asks for the quoted price; false keeps the rate as entered. `SalesLineBuilder.BuildAsync(drafts, customer, …)` prices auto lines through `PricingService.ContextAsync(customer)`. Every item line stores `ListRate` (the item rate then) and `PriceSource` (the quote's steps, `SalesLine.ManualPrice` when typed and different, null at list); `Saved` = list amount − amount. Copies (`SalesLine.Drafts`) keep the price and source, so invoicing an estimate doesn't reprice. Recurring invoice templates store fixed rates.
- `SalesLinesEditor` takes `CustomerId` and `Date`, loads the context and last prices through `Operations`, reprices auto lines on every change, and turns auto off when a rate is typed ("Automatic price" turns it back on). `PriceNote` shows list price and source on saved documents; `FormService.Savings` gives the "You saved" row on screens and PDFs (invoices, estimates, receipts).
- `PricingService`: level/rule CRUD with audit (`price_level`, `price_rule`), quantity breaks (audited on the item), rounding (audited on the company), per item level CSV import (Item + Price columns, all or nothing) and export, `QuoteAsync`, `LastPricesAsync` (posted invoices and receipts). Grids, uploads and rounding post to `Web/Endpoints/PricingEndpoints.cs` (`IFormCollection`); rounding needs the accountant role. API under `/api/pricing`.
- Reports: `price-list` (customer or level, as of a date; `ReportInputs.PriceFor`) and `discounts-given` (by pricing source, or each line) in `PricingReports`.
- Static forms: a multi-select with nothing chosen binds as null; guard array properties (`set => field = value ?? []`). Numbers in plain form fields go through `FormNumbers` (Web/Endpoints): blank is null, unreadable text is refused, never read as blank.

## Everyday depth (2026-11-02)

- Copy: new-document pages take `?copy={id}` (invoices, estimates, sales receipts, sales orders, bills not from a purchase order, spends/receives `/banking/{account}/record/{kind}?copy=`, purchase orders, journal entries) and fill the form, dated today with the next number. `SalesLineModel.CopiesOf` / `BillLineModel.CopiesOf` leave out tax lines, lines that billed particular costs, time or mileage (by `PriceSource`) and lots. `?item={id}` starts invoices, receipts, estimates and purchase orders with a line for the item (`SalesLineModel.ForItem`; the Item Center's "New transaction").

A refusal is re-thrown as "{customer}: …".
