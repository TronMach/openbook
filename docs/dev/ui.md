# Simple view, navigation and pickers

Developer notes moved out of `CLAUDE.md` (index there). Read this file before changing this area; keep it up to date in the same commit.

## Simple view (L14)

- `ViewMode` (Domain/Companies/ViewMode.cs: `Full` / `Simple`): a person's own choice is a `ViewPreference` row keyed by username (not audited, like dashboard panels); otherwise `Company.DefaultViewMode` (audited in the company snapshot; chosen at `/setup`, which suggests Simple; Settings → View; `PUT /api/company`; existing companies backfilled `Full`). `ViewModeService.CurrentAsync` gives the `ViewState` (mode, edition); the link in the top bar or menu bar posts `view` and `returnUrl` to `POST /account/view`. Code says "view", never "edition" (L11) or "install" (L13). Like editions it only changes what's shown: never make data, rules or access depend on it, and never hide a page from someone who'd otherwise reach it.
- `MainLayout` renders the simple view as `SimpleNav` (menu bar of `<details class="menu">` drop-downs; one open at a time, Esc and outside clicks close them, by `shortcuts.js`) and `SimpleShortcuts` (shortcut column with our own line icons, a row on phones) around the page. Every label is written out as `L["…"]` in the component (the extractor only reads razor literals); links go through `CanReach`, and a menu with nothing reachable isn't shown. Each edition adds its lead area (time, gifts, sales channels) and its word for customers.
- `TodoList BesideWorkflow` under the workflow leaves out what the steps count and draws nothing when nothing else waits; Getting started shows in both views.
- Home page in the simple view: `WorkflowHome` (Components/Home) draws the steps as a CSS grid with SVG arrows (vendors: purchase orders → receive inventory → bills → pay bills; customers: estimates → invoices → payments → deposits, sales receipts into deposits; company and banking lists), each a link to start the work with what's waiting from `HomeWorkflowService.FiguresAsync(today)` (`WorkflowFigures`; bills awaiting approval don't count as payable; due soon = within `DueSoonDays`). Every step stays visible; one never used says what it's for. `/banking/write-checks` opens the spend form of the only bank account, else asks which.
- Familiar, not copied: borrow QuickBooks Desktop's organization and generic task names ("Create Invoices", "Pay Bills"), never Intuit's icons, artwork, colors, logos, wording or the QuickBooks name beyond saying what we import from.
- Centers (slice 2): `/customers/center`, `/vendors/center`, `/items/center` (static, `?id=&q=&showInactive=&type=&dates=`; no id = the first listed; detail first on phones). Lists come from `CustomerService`/`VendorService.ListAsync` (balances) and `InventoryService.StockAsync`; transactions from `CenterService` (Application/Centers: `CenterRow` per document with its audit entity type, linked through `Links.ForAuditEntity`, `CenterState`, open amount; items: documents with lines for it plus stock movements of other sources grouped by source), shown and filtered by `CenterTransactions` (Components/Centers), which labels each entity type (add a label for a new document type there). "New transaction" links pass `?customer=` / `?vendor=` presets (the sales receipt form takes one now). `details.menu` drop-downs anywhere close one another and on Esc or an outside click.
- Simpler forms (slice 3): `<MoreOptions Open=… HasContent=… Title=…>` (Components) renders its fields as they are in the full view and folds them into `<details class="more-options">` in the simple view (it asks `ViewModeService` through `Operations`, so it works on static and interactive pages), open when a field inside isn't at its default; `HasContent` false draws nothing extra when the fields hide themselves. Used for deposit accounts (payments, receipts; default Undeposited Funds), location/warehouse (invoices, receipts, estimates, credit memos, bills), the item form (income, stock accounts, tracking, costing, auto-build; plain question labels in the simple view), customers (pricing, sub-customer, collections; sales tax open when tax codes exist) and vendors (1099). Error messages on everyday forms avoid ledger terms ("kept in a different account for money owed to you").
- Setup, reports and dashboard (slice 4): `/setup` asks what they sell (`StarterSales`), whether they keep stock and charge sales tax; starting in the simple view without an import, the page applies `ChartTemplates.ApplyAsync(key, StarterAnswers)` (the business type's template trimmed by `ChartTemplates.Csv(key, answers)`: by account name, plus "1400 Inventory Asset" in the four-column charts; audited as the new owner) and goes home, or to `/sales-tax?welcome=true`; if the chart can't be applied, to `/accounts`. `/reports` in the simple view lists `ReportsHub.Everyday` and links to `?all=true`; `/dashboard` in the simple view relabels the position and profit panels and leaves out `Dashboard.FullViewOnly` (ratios, month-end cash), posting those panels' current choice as hidden fields so the full view keeps them.
- Guide chapter 28. Not done: an API for a person's own view; an item preset on new forms; the expense form's location stays inline.

## Usability pass (2026-11-02)

- Access on pages: link to a page with `<ActionLink Href=… Class=…>` and post with `<PostForm Action=…>` (Components), never a bare `<a>`/`<form method="post" action>` for anything that may need more access. Allowed: an ordinary link/form. Not allowed: greyed out (`a.unavailable`; the form's controls in a disabled `fieldset.unavailable-form`), with `AccessRules.NeedsText` as the tooltip/note, linking to `AccessRules.DeniedUrl` (the access denied page names the active admins and goes `back`). Nothing is hidden on pages, and it works again once access is given. Menus still hide what people can't reach (`CanReach`). Component attributes can't mix text and C#: write `Href="@($"/x/{id}")"`.
- `/settings` is an index anyone may open (`AccessRules.IsSettingsIndex`: ViewOnly, no area); every other `/settings/*` path keeps its rule. Each entry has a sentence and a status line worked out only when the person can reach it; a new settings page gets an entry there (and keywords for the filter box). The company form is `/settings/company` (`CompanySettings.razor`; "Legal structure" = `BusinessType`, "Which menu suits your work?" = edition).
- Help: `docs/user-guide/*.md` is copied beside the app (`help/`, csproj `None` item; the Dockerfile copies `docs/user-guide`) and shown at `/help` and `/help/{chapter}` by `Help/HelpGuide` (Markdig 0.38.0, BSD-2-Clause, raw HTML disabled; chapter links rewritten to `/help`, `../` links to GitHub; `?q=` searches). The top bar's **Help** goes to `HelpTopics.For(path)` (first matching prefix): add a prefix there for a new area. `<HelpLink Chapter=… Section=…>` links a page to a section. `g` then `?` opens it.
- Top bar: menu, Settings, search, Help, and the account menu under the username (password and sessions, two-factor, offline capture, language, theme, view switch, menu style, sign out). `MenuStyle` (`Bar` default, `DropDowns`) is `ViewPreference.Menu`, per person (`ViewModeService.SetMenuAsync`, `POST /account/menu`); `ViewPreference.Mode` is now null when someone has only chosen a menu style. `FullMenu` draws the full view's drop-downs (each label written out; links hidden by `CanReach`, dividers only between links).
- Area pages put their links under the title in `.hub-actions` groups (Create, Lists, Tools, Reports, Set up); keep one or two primary buttons beside the title.
- Full-view home: `Components/Home/TodoList` (what's waiting, each line only when there's something and the person can reach it; a getting-started checklist that disappears when complete). Filter boxes: `<input data-filter="#list">` over `[data-filter-item]`/`[data-filter-group]`/`[data-filter-empty]`; form parts shown for some choices: `data-shown-by="#select" data-shown-for="A B"` (both in `shortcuts.js`).
- Phones: under 760px tables scroll inside themselves and toolbars wrap (app.css); check new pages at 375px for sideways scrolling.

## Pickers

- Interactive forms choose customers, vendors, items, categories and accounts with `SearchSelect` (Components/Search): type any part of a name (every word must match the label, detail or keywords, ignoring case and accents; names starting with the text first), arrows and Enter to choose (`wwwroot/searchselect.js`), Esc or Tab to leave; leaving with an exact name typed chooses it. Build options with `PickerOptions.Customers/Vendors/Items/Accounts` (accounts grouped by class, searchable by number) and cache them per source list in editors. Bind with `@bind-Value` (Guid?) or `OnChange` for handlers written for a select's `ChangeEventArgs`; `EmptyText` adds a "none" choice. Plain `<select>` is still right for short fixed lists (kinds, classes, frequencies).
- `SearchSelect` with `CustomerAdded`, `VendorAdded` or `ItemAdded` (the callback puts the record in the page's list) adds a "+ Add" row for people who can reach that `/…/new` page; `QuickAdd` asks only the basics and the picker chooses the result. `SalesLinesEditor` takes `OnItemAdded`. So the new-document forms draw with empty lists, and a first invoice can be finished on the form. Items made there are services or products not counted in stock (`ItemService.CreateQuickAsync`); the simple setup adds a "Services" item (`CreateStarterServiceAsync`) unless they only sell products.
- Static (server-rendered) forms get the same picker by adding `data-search` to a `<select>`/`InputSelect` (`searchselect.js`): a search box goes in front and the hidden select still carries the value, so the form works without script. An empty-value option is the "none" choice, optgroups become headings, `data-keywords` on an option is searched too. It's re-added after every enhanced load; never use it on interactive pages (use `SearchSelect`) or on multi-selects. A grid with one long list on every row gives each select only its current choice and `data-options="#id"` naming one `<datalist>`, so the page isn't rows × options long (reorder, document review); picking another then needs the script.
- Typing (2026-11-11): the picker's box is bound (`@bind:get="_text" @bind:set="Typed" @bind:event="oninput"`). It was `value="@_text"` with `@oninput`, and Blazor protects a field's typed text only for bindings (the event's `UpdatesAttributeName`): each keystroke's render wrote that keystroke's text back into the box, so characters typed meanwhile were lost (found by the browser tests at full typing speed; a barcode scanner, or anyone on a slow connection, would hit it). `BrowserTests.A_barcode_scanned_into_the_item_picker_arrives_whole` fails without the binding.
