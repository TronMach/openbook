## Inventory (V1.6)

- `ItemType.Inventory` items carry `InventoryAssetAccountId` (other current asset), `CostAccountId` (expense class, usually COGS), `ReorderPoint` and `PurchaseCost`. `ItemService` fills missing accounts with "Inventory Asset" / "Cost of Goods Sold" (`InventoryAccounts`, created on first use; "Inventory Shrinkage" is the default adjustment account). Once an item has movements its type and asset account are locked.
- Stock is an append-only ledger of `InventoryMovement`s (Domain/Inventory). A receipt is its own FIFO layer (`LayerId == Id`); issues and reversals point at a layer. `Fifo.Layers` derives remaining quantity/cost; `Fifo.Take` uses the oldest layers received on or before the date, proportional cost rounded to the cent with the last of a layer taking exactly what's left. Stock can't go negative (`inventory.insufficient`) unless the item may be sold short (below).
- Documents move stock through `InventoryService` in three steps: plan (checks everything: `PlanSaleAsync`, `PlanReturnAsync`, `PlanPurchaseAsync`, `PlanReversalAsync`), stage the entry with the plan's postings appended, then `Apply`. Sales post Dr COGS / Cr inventory asset in their own entry; credit memos restock at the latest sale's average unit cost (else average on hand, else purchase cost). Voids add opposite movements on the document date and are refused once a layer the document opened has been used (`inventory.layer_used`). `InventorySources` names the document types (audit entity types).
- Bills take item lines (`BillLineDraft.ItemId`/`Quantity`): the line posts to the item's asset account and opens a layer at the line amount. Recurring bills refuse item lines. Vendor credits take them to send stock back (`InventoryService.PlanVendorReturnAsync`, movements `InventorySources.VendorCredit`): the line credits the asset at the credit amount, the stock leaves at its cost, the difference goes to "Purchase Price Variance" (`PurchaseAccounts.GetOrCreatePriceVarianceAsync`); no lot/serial items (`vendorcredit.tracked_item`); such a credit can't be changed (`vendorcredit.items_fixed`), and its void reverses the stock. `BillLinesEditor` shows item columns when given `Items` (bills only).
- `InventoryAdjustment` (`Source = inventory.adjustment`, audit `inventory_adjustment`, attachments allowed): increases at a unit cost, decreases at FIFO cost, write-downs (`InventoryAdjustmentLineDraft.ValueChange` < 0 with quantity 0: `CostAdjustment` movements over the layers on hand by remaining cost, `inventory.write_down_too_much`); value change against the adjustment account (Opening Balance Equity for starting stock; `AdjustmentAccountId = Guid.Empty` creates it, since a new company doesn't have it).
- Cash basis keeps the inventory cost lines of invoices and credit memos as posted (`ReportLedger`): COGS is recognized when stock is sold.
- A sale short on its date first takes stock dated later (`Costing.Take`, only for items that may be sold short), at its real cost; so a past date's movements can hold an issue whose receipt comes later, and `Fifo.Layers`/`Slices` keep such a layer, at what's been taken, so past stock adds up. Only what's beyond all the stock is a shortfall.
- Selling short: `Company.AllowSellingShort` (Settings → Stock, audited in the company snapshot) and `Item.SellShort` (`ShortStock.Company/Allow/Never`; `Allow` refused for lot/serial items, `item.short_stock_tracked`); `Item.CanSellShort(companyAllows)` decides. `InventoryService.ShortfallCostAsync` gives the estimate (`Costing.EstimatedUnitCost`: purchase cost, else latest receipt's unit cost, else average, else 0) or null; sales (the automatic take) and build components pass it to `TakeAndSimulate`/`Costing.Take`, which takes what `Fifo.Available` allows and makes the rest one `FifoTake` with `Shortfall` → `InventoryMovement.Issue` writes it as a shortfall layer (a receipt of −q at −cost, `IsShortfall`). Transfers and adjustment decreases never sell short. Every receipt goes through `InventoryService.Receive` (receipts made with `PlannedReceipt`, then `ForSource`): it fills open shortfalls oldest first, same warehouse first (`InventoryMovement.Cover` +q at the estimate on the shortfall, an issue −q at actual cost from the receipt) and posts the difference Dr/Cr cost of goods sold vs the asset in the receiving document's entry, line memo starting `InventoryService.ShortfallMemo` (cash basis keeps those bill lines as posted). Bills and PO receipts now stage the stock plan's postings; adjustments add them as `corrections`; auto-builds inside a sale plan with `fill: false`. A sale whose shortfall was filled can't be voided (`inventory.shortfall_filled`); voiding the filling document reopens it.
- `EfInventoryStore.ListMovementsAsync` includes unsaved added movements, so several documents in one save see each other's stock.
- Reports: `inventory-valuation` (as of a date, summary or layers, with a tie-out row to the asset accounts), `inventory-stock` (reorder status) and `inventory-aging` (value on hand by layer age: 90/180/365 days and older, last sold date from invoice, receipt and channel issues; longest unsold first) in `ReportCatalog`, built by `InventoryReports`.

- Physical counts (2026-11-03): `InventoryAdjustmentService.CountAsync(InventoryCountCommand)` compares counts with the warehouse's stock on the date and makes one adjustment for the differences (reference "Physical count"; increases at purchase cost into no bin, decreases from any bin); null when all matched; lot/serial items refused (`count.tracked`). Report `physical-inventory-worksheet` (`InventoryReports.CountSheetAsync`, lots listed). Page `/inventory/count` posts `count:{itemId}` to `/inventory/count` (`InventoryEndpoints`); API `POST /api/inventory/count`.
- Items spreadsheet (2026-11-08): `ItemFileService` (Application/Inventory/ItemFile.cs) exports every item as one CSV (Item ID, details, accounts by full name, Price, Purchase cost, `Quantity breaks` as "10: 5%; 50: 8.00", a `Price level: {name}` column per active per-item level, On hand in one warehouse, a column per active item custom field (stored form; `Field: {name}` when the name clashes with a built-in header)) and takes it back: rows match by Item ID else name, else new; absent columns change nothing; a blank cell keeps type, active, prices, taxable, accounts, cost method and tracking and clears the rest; a changed On hand becomes one `CountAsync` adjustment (reference `AdjustmentReference`, `ItemFileOptions` date, warehouse, account: null shrinkage, `Guid.Empty` Opening Balance Equity). `PreviewAsync` plans each row (`ItemFileRow` status New/Changed/Unchanged/Error with change texts), then runs the import in a transaction it never commits (`ItemFilePlan.Problem`); `ImportAsync` is all or nothing through `ItemService`, `PricingService` and `CustomFieldService` (their audits). A new item field needs a column here. Page `/inventory/items/import` (interactive), download `GET /inventory/items.csv` (UTF-8 with BOM, `ItemFiles.Bytes`), API `GET|POST /api/inventory/items/file` (`dryRun`, `date`, `warehouseId`, `adjustmentAccountId`). Tests: `ItemFileTests`.
- Batch change (2026-11-13): `ItemBatchService` (Application/Inventory/ItemBatch.cs) makes one `ItemBatchChange` (type Service/NonInventory/Inventory only, category (blank clears), income account, taxable, active, price set or percent rounded to the cent, `MaxPercent` 1000) to up to `MaxItems` (5000) items through `ItemService.UpdateAsync`/`SetActiveAsync` (their audits). Rows are Changed, Unchanged or Skipped with the reason (`Item.HasComponents`, stock history, a component of an assembly turning untracked, `Item.EnsureIncomeAccount` for the new type); skipped items are left out and the rest saved in one transaction; nothing to change throws `item_batch.nothing_to_change`. `Versions` (each item's version at review) refuse a changed item with `save.stale`, naming it. Items becoming inventory products may get `StartingStock` (quantity, unit cost or their purchase cost): one `InventoryAdjustmentService.CreateAsync` against `Guid.Empty` (Opening Balance Equity, else the first equity account), reference `AdjustmentReference` ("Batch change"), default warehouse, `StockDate` or today. `PreviewAsync` plans, tries and rolls back (`ItemBatchPlan.Problem`); both it and a failed `ApplyAsync` call `IUnitOfWork.DiscardPending`, since rolled-back values stay on the tracked items. `Item.Details()` gives an item's `ItemDetails` (the spreadsheet uses it too). Page `/items/batch` (static, one `@formname="batch"` form bound as `Input`, steps choose → review → apply; the list posts the ticked `Input.Ids` with `_handler=batch`); starting stock only for someone who `CanPost("/inventory/adjustments/new")`, since the page is in the sales area. `MaxFormMappingCollectionSize` is raised to `MaxItems` in Program.cs (Blazor's default of 1,024 would stop a long tick list). API `POST /api/items/batch` (`dryRun`; `ItemBatchRequest` without starting stock). Tests: `ItemBatchTests`, `ApiAccessTests.Many_items_change_at_once_through_the_api`, `BrowserTests.Ticked_products_become_inventory_products_with_their_starting_stock`.

## Inventory costing (L4, slice 2)

- `Item.CostMethod` (`InventoryCostMethod.Fifo` / `Average`, tracked items only, locked once stock has moved). Every stock issue goes through `Costing.Take(item, movements, quantity, date)`: quantity still comes out of FIFO layers (so availability and void checks are unchanged), but average items cost the take at the average on hand on the date (rounded once, spread over the layers; taking everything takes its whole value). Layer costs of average items aren't meaningful one by one; the valuation detail shows one "Weighted average" row for them.
- `ItemType.Bundle` (kit) and `ItemType.Assembly`; `Item.IsInventory` (= `Item.IsTracked(type)`) is true for inventory products and assemblies. Both have `Components` (`ItemComponent`, owned table `ItemComponents`), set with `ItemService.SetComponentsAsync`: bundles take any non-bundle item; assemblies take tracked items only, no cycles. A component's type can't change in a way its parent forbids.
- Bundles sell as one sales line (their own price and income account); `InventoryService.ItemQuantitiesAsync` expands them into tracked components (line quantity × component quantity) for sales, returns and voids, costing to each component's accounts.
- `AssemblyBuild` (`Source = inventory.build`, audit `assembly_build`): `AssemblyBuildService` issues the bill of materials × quantity at each component's cost and receives one layer of the assembly at the total (Dr assembly asset, Cr component assets). Void = unbuild, refused once the built layer has been used. `PreviewAsync` gives needs, on hand and the most buildable.
- `LandedCost` (`Source = inventory.landed_cost`, audit `landed_cost`): spreads an amount from an account (default "Landed Cost Clearing", other current asset, created on first use; never bank, card, A/R or A/P) over the receipts of chosen posted bills, by value or quantity (`LandedCost.Allocate`, by `Shares.ByWeight`; receipts ordered by item then id). Each share splits by the layer's remaining quantity: that part becomes an `InventoryMovement.CostAdjustment` (quantity 0) on the layer and Dr the item's asset; the rest Dr its cost of goods sold (stock built into assemblies counts as gone, too). A bill under a landed cost can't be voided; a landed cost can't be voided once its layers have moved since.
- Pending builds (`PendingBuild`, audit `pending_build`): a build saved to finish later posts nothing and holds no stock. `AssemblyBuildService`: `SavePendingAsync`, `BuildWhatYouCanAsync` (builds the most whole units the warehouse allows, the rest pending, one save; output lots are for the built part), `FinishPendingAsync` (an ordinary build on the finish date), `CancelPendingAsync`, `ListPendingAsync` (with today's preview). The build page posts `Input.Mode` = build / partial / pending / finish; `?pending={id}` finishes one. Builds are planned by `InventoryService.PlanBuildAsync`, which also serves auto-builds.
- `Item.AutoBuild` (assemblies without lot or serial tracking): `PlanSaleAsync` builds a sale's shortfall in the warehouse from components inside the sale's own entry (Dr assembly asset, Cr components) before taking the sale; the build's movements belong to the sale, so voiding it undoes both. Receipts planned before a document exists are made with a placeholder source and re-sourced with `InventoryMovement.ForSource` (same id). `PlanReversalAsync` counts a layer as used only when movements from other documents (not voided again) touch it.
- Pages `/inventory/builds/new` (preview by query string, then an `EditForm`), `/inventory/builds/{id}`, `/inventory/landed-costs/new`, `/inventory/landed-costs/{id}`; components grid on the item page posts to `/items/{id}/components` (`Web/Endpoints/InventoryEndpoints.cs`). API under `/api/inventory` (`/items/{id}/components`, `/builds`, `/landed-costs`).

## Stock tracking (L4, slice 3)

- A warehouse or bin with stock stays active (`warehouse.has_stock`, `bin.has_stock`). An invoice or receipt whose shortfall has been filled can still be corrected while its items, quantities, lots, date and warehouse stay: its movements are kept and the new entry repeats the old cost lines (`InventoryService.KeptStockAsync`). Found stock on a count uses `Costing.EstimatedUnitCost`. `form[data-keep-typed]` (`shortcuts.js`) brings a form's values back after a refusal (the gift, pledge, grant and count forms; nothing of type password).
- Places: `Warehouse` (one `IsDefault`, "Main", created on first use by `WarehouseService.DefaultAsync`) and `Bin` (in a warehouse). Documents and movements store the default warehouse as **null** (`Warehouse.Key`), so pre-warehouse data needs no backfill; `StockPlace(WarehouseId, BinId)` is a place. `WarehouseService.CheckAsync` turns a chosen id or place into the stored form (active, bin in that warehouse). Deactivated, never deleted; the default can't be.
- `InventoryMovement` carries `WarehouseId`, `BinId` and `Lot` (set on the receipt, copied onto everything that moves it). Costing is still per item across places: `Fifo.Slices` splits each layer by place, and `Fifo.Take(movements, quantity, date, name, StockFilter)` takes from slices the filter allows (`StockFilter.In(warehouse)` = any bin; `At(place)` = exactly that bin or no bin; `.WithLot`). `Costing.Take` passes the filter. Plans that take an item more than once use `InventoryService.TakeAndSimulate` so later takes see earlier ones.
- Invoices, sales receipts, credit memos and bills have `WarehouseId` (commands take `WarehouseId`; `WarehouseField` on the forms, shown once there are two warehouses). Sales take from any bin of the warehouse; receipts arrive outside bins. Adjustment lines take a place (`StockPlaces` lists warehouses and bins for static forms) and lots; builds take a warehouse (components and output) and output lots.
- `StockTransfer` (audit `stock_transfer`, `InventorySources.Transfer`) moves stock between places on the same layers (`InventoryMovement.Transfer`: −q at the source, +q at the destination, cost 0); posts nothing, but its date must be open (`EnsureOpen`), as for a void. Void refused once the moved stock has left the destination. A bill/receipt void is refused once its layer has moved anywhere (`PlanReversalAsync` checks the receipt's place too).
- Lots and serials: `Item.Tracking` (`ItemTracking.None/Lot/Serial`, tracked items only, locked once stock has moved). `LotText` parses "A-1 x 3; B-2 x 2" (one lot alone = the whole line) and serials "S1, S2" (whole units, one each) and formats them back. Each lot or serial is its own receipt layer (cost split by quantity, `InventoryService.Split`). Receipts must name them (`lot.required`); a serial can't be received while in stock. Sales take the lots named on the line, else the oldest; the lots actually used are saved on `SalesLine.Lots` / `BillLine.Lots` (`RecordLots`) and shown on screens and PDFs. Bundle components always take the oldest. `InventoryService.LotHistoryAsync` and the inventory page's lookup trace a lot or serial.
- Lot expiry: `LotDetail` (item + lot, `ExpiresOn`), set on the item page or `InventoryService.SetLotExpiryAsync` (lot-tracked items, existing lots). Automatic picks go through `InventoryService.ForPickingAsync`, which adds the expiries to the `StockFilter`: soonest expiry first, expired lots skipped (a named lot is still taken). `ExpiringAsync` lists lots on hand expiring by a date (inventory page: next 30 days).
- Landed costs follow stock into assemblies: the share for a layer's quantity issued to (unvoided) builds goes to the build's assembly layers by quantity, split again into on hand (cost adjustment) and sold (the assembly's cost of goods sold); only what was sold or adjusted goes to the component's cost of goods sold. One level deep.
- Labels: a barcode whose narrowest bar would be under `LabelService.NarrowestBarPoints` (0.54 pt) across `PrintableWidthPoints` is refused (`label.barcode_too_long`). `Code128` (Application/Inventory/Labels.cs: set C for even digit runs of 4+, else set B; `Modules` gives bar/space widths) and `LabelService` → `ILabelRenderer` (`LabelRenderer`, PDFsharp, Letter 3 × 10 of 2⅝" × 1"). Page `/inventory/labels` posts to `/inventory/labels` (PDF). Don't name a page `Labels.razor`: its class hides the `Labels` helper.
- Reports: `ReportInputs.Warehouse` (`warehouseId`, a warehouse's id) limits `inventory-valuation` (detail by bin and lot; no tie-out row) and `inventory-stock` to one warehouse.
- Barcodes: `Item.Barcode` (unique ignoring case, plain printable, ≤ 50) is a picker keyword, so scanning into an item `SearchSelect` and pressing Enter picks it; `ItemService.FindByBarcodeAsync`, API `/api/inventory/items/barcode/{code}`.
- Pages `/inventory/warehouses` (form posts in `InventoryEndpoints`), `/inventory/transfers/new`, `/inventory/transfers/{id}`; stock by place and lot on item pages. API under `/api/inventory` (`/warehouses`, `/bins`, `/stock/places`, `/lots/{lot}`, `/transfers`). Audit types `warehouse`, `stock_transfer`.
