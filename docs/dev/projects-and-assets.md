# Projects, time, fixed assets and loans

Developer notes moved out of `CLAUDE.md` (index there). Read this file before changing this area; keep it up to date in the same commit.

## Projects and time (L5, slice 1)

- Domain in `Domain/Projects/Projects.cs`. `Project` is one row per customer (`CustomerId` unique): the project is a customer record, usually a sub-customer billed with its parent (`ProjectService.CreateAsync` makes one inside an `IUnitOfWork` transaction, copying the parent's terms, tax code and price level), so invoices, receipts and credit memos need nothing new. Status, dates, revenue/cost/hours budgets. Audit `project`.
- `Customer.TimeBillingFormat` (`EachEntry` / `ByPerson` / `ByService` / `OneLine`, text; `ProjectService.SetTimeBillingFormatAsync`, the same card and form field `timeFormat`, API `ResponsibleRequest.TimeBillingFormat`): `ProjectBilling.DraftsAsync(…, format)` sums time per owner, service, class (and person or rate), billing hours × rate when that's exactly the entries' total, else the total once; `FormService` adds an "Hours" fact from `IProjectStore.ListTimeForInvoiceAsync`.
- `Customer.ResponsibleWorkerId` (no foreign key; `CustomerTree.Responsible` walks up the tree; `ProjectService.SetResponsibleAsync`/`ResponsibleForAsync`, audited `Updated` on the customer; `<ResponsibleCard>` on customer and project pages posts `/customers/{id}/responsible`, API `PUT /api/customers/{id}/responsible`): `ReportInputs.Responsible` (`responsibleId`) filters `customer-balances`, `project-profitability`, `unbilled-work`, `unbilled-aging` (WIP by age, from the same pieces: `ProjectReports.UnbilledPiecesAsync`) and `time-by-customer`, which also takes `ReportInputs.Person` (`workerId`).
- `Worker.ClassId` (optional department): `ProjectBilling` puts billed time's invoice lines on it, so income by class isn't "Not specified".
- `Worker` (audit `worker`; `/time/people` and `/api/projects/workers` need the accountant level to change, and below it cost rates are hidden on that page and null in worker and time responses, `ProjectsApi.SeesCosts`): name (unique ignoring case), cost rate, optional bill rate, optional `Username` (one active worker per user; `WorkerService.ForCurrentUserAsync`) and vendor. Deactivated, never deleted.
- `TimeEntry` (audit `time_entry`): hours (2 decimals, ≤ 24), optional customer and service (`Service`/`NonInventory` item; both required when billable), bill rate (given, else the worker's, else the item's rate) and the worker's cost rate copied when entered. A timer is an entry with `StartedAt` and no hours; `StopAsync` sets the rounded minutes; one running per worker. Editable and deletable until invoiced. Time posts nothing.
- `MileageReimbursementService.PayBackAsync(workerId, through, billDate)` (one transaction): a bill to the worker's `VendorId` for their trips not paid back (`MileageTrip.ReimbursedBillId` null or its bill voided; `OwedAsync`), each at its year's rate, on "Mileage Reimbursement" (expense, created on first use); form `POST /mileage/pay-back`.
- `MileageTrip` (audit `mileage_trip`): miles (1 decimal), purpose, optional customer, billable per mile. `MileageRate` rows (key = year) override `MileageRate.Standard` (IRS business rates); `MileageService.RatesAsync` merges them. Rates need the accountant role.
- Billing: `CreateInvoiceCommand.TimeEntryIds` / `MileageTripIds` → `ProjectBilling` (checked like billable expenses: same customer, billable, not invoiced) adds lines (time: its service, hours × rate, price source "Billable time"; mileage: the "Mileage" service item on "Billable Expense Income", untaxed) and sets `InvoiceId`; `InvoiceService.VoidAsync` releases them.
- `Customer.DefaultMarkupPercent` (column `DefaultMarkupTenThousandths`; `ProjectService.SetDefaultMarkupAsync`, the customer card's `markup`): `MarkBillableCommand.MarkupPercent` null takes it; `BillableExpenseService.MarkAllAsync` marks every unmarked line of a bill or expense in one save (`POST /sales/billable/all`, "Mark them all"); the new bill and expense forms' `BillableToField` (Components/Purchases) calls it in the same `IUnitOfWork` transaction as the save.
- `BilledTimeService.TakeBackAsync(TakeBackTimeCommand)` (one transaction): a credit memo for chosen entries of a posted invoice (hours × `RateBilled`, price source billable time, the invoice's tax code), applied up to the invoice's balance, the entries' `InvoiceId` cleared and, with `WriteOff`, adjusted to 0. `<TakeBackTime>` on posted invoices posts `/sales/invoices/{id}/take-back-time` (`entry` repeated, `date`, `reason`, `writeOff`).
- `BillableExpense.IsBillable` false = a project cost only (no markup, never invoiced; existing rows were backfilled true). The bill/expense panel's "Bill it" box sets it.
- `ProjectReports.FiguresAsync`: income = posted invoices + receipts − credit memos without tax lines; costs = marked bill/expense lines + time × cost rate + miles × the year's rate; unbilled = billable not invoiced. `WithSubProjectsAsync` adds every project below it in the customer tree (`ProjectFigures.Plus`; null without any); `BudgetWarnings()` from 90% (`WarnAtPercent`) of the cost or hours budget. New time and trips on a Completed or Cancelled project are refused (`project.closed_for_work`, `ProjectService.EnsureOpenForWorkAsync`). Reports `project-profitability`, `time-by-customer`, `mileage-log` (group "Projects and time").
- Billing run (`PreBillService`, `/time/billing`, drafts `/time/billing/{id}`): a worksheet of unbilled work through a date by who's billed (`CustomerTree.BillTo`), `PreBill` drafts (audit `pre_bill`; posts nothing, lines hold what's worked and what's billed; work on a draft stays off other drafts), `PostAsync` writes changed amounts onto the time and makes the invoice in one transaction, `PostManyAsync` goes on after a refusal and can email. `TimeEntry.Adjust`, and the same `Adjust` on `MileageTrip` (`Worked`, `RateBilled`) and `BillableExpense` (`Worked`; `Amount` = billed else worked) for a draft's mileage and cost lines (0 makes them not billable; audited `Updated` on each) (`BilledAmount`, reason, date, who; `Worked` is hours at the rate, `Amount` what's billed, `RateBilled` the invoice line's rate; 0 is `WrittenOff`, which leaves the unbilled list). `Project.Arrangement` (`FixedFee`, `NotToExceed`) and `Fee`: fixed-fee time stays out of unbilled work, `OpenAsync` and the worksheet; a capped project warns there. Reports `realization`, `time-write-downs`.
- Time defaults: `Company.TimeBillableByDefault` (Time form and new week-grid rows) and `TimeIncrementMinutes` (`Company.TimeIncrements`; `TimeEntry.RoundUpTo` on `TimeService.CreateAsync` and `StopAsync`); `SetDefaultsAsync`/`DefaultsAsync`, form `POST /time/defaults` (accountant).
- Time lock: `Company.TimeLockedThrough` (`TimeService.SetLockAsync`/`LockedThroughAsync`, audited `Updated` on the company; form `POST /time/lock` (accountant), API `GET|PUT /api/projects/time/lock`): `time.locked` on adding, changing (either date), deleting or a week grid change dated through it; billing, write-downs and repricing still work. Weeks handed in: `TimeSheet` (Domain/Projects/TimeSheets.cs; table `TimeSheets`, unique per worker and Monday; Submitted → Approved / Returned, returned ones resubmitted; audit `time_sheet`, linked to `/time/review`): `TimeService.SubmitWeekAsync` (refused with no time or a running timer), `ApproveSheetAsync`, `ReturnSheetAsync` (note required; also from Approved), `SheetsAsync(status)`; a submitted or approved week refuses time changes (`time.submitted`, checked with the lock by worker). Week grid `POST /time/week/submit`; page `/time/review` (accountant to decide) posts `/time/review/{id}/approve|return`; API `GET|POST /api/projects/time/sheets`, `/api/projects/time-review` (+ `/{id}/approve|return`, accountant). `TimeService.MissingAsync(monday)` (active people with `WeeklyHours` who entered fewer; the Time page's "Missing time last week", API `/api/projects/time/missing`).
- Week grid `/time/week` (`TimeService.WeekAsync` / `SaveWeekAsync` with `WeekRow`s: client, service, billable, hours Monday–Sunday; one save; rows for the same work add up; an empty cell gets an entry, a one-entry cell changes or (0) is deleted, a cell with several entries or invoiced time is locked; null leaves a day; posts `c:r`, `i:r`, `b:r`, `h:r:d`, `n:r` (`WeekRow.Notes`, for the entries the save adds) to `/time/week`, and `new:r` on the blank rows, whose empty cells mean null where a saved row's mean 0; `?copy=true` fills the blank rows from `TimeService.RowsFromLastWeekAsync`).
- Pages `/projects`, `/projects/{id}`, `/time` (timers, add time, filters), `/time/{id}/edit`, `/time/people`, `/mileage`; the new invoice form ticks billable time and trips. Form posts in `Web/Endpoints/ProjectEndpoints.cs`; API under `/api/projects` (`/workers`, `/time`, `/time/start`, `/mileage`, `/mileage-rates`).

## Fixed assets and loans (L5, slice 2)

- Domain in `Domain/Assets`. `FixedAsset` (audit `fixed_asset`): asset, accumulated depreciation and expense accounts (`Guid.Empty` in the details = "Accumulated Depreciation" / "Depreciation Expense", created by `AssetAccounts` on first use), cost, salvage, in-service date, `DepreciationMethod` (StraightLine, DecliningBalance with `DecliningPercent`, Macrs with `MacrsClass`, None), and `PriorDepreciation` through `PriorThrough` for assets bought before OpenBook. Creating one posts nothing (the purchase is a bill/expense/journal entry); one entered by mistake goes to `FixedAssetStatus.Removed` (`RemoveAsync`, refused once depreciation is posted; `PutBackAsync`), left out of runs and reports. Once depreciation is posted only name, number and description can change.
- `Depreciation.Schedule` gives one amount per calendar month from the in-service month, adding up exactly to `Base` (cost − salvage; MACRS ignores salvage) by cumulative rounding. MACRS: Pub 946 half-year tables (3–20 year), Table A-7a held for 39-year (its figures differ slightly from the fraction), mid-month formula for 27.5-year (equal to Table A-6 to three places); each tax year (calendar years) spread evenly over its months. `FixedAsset.FirstYearAmount` (section 179 or bonus, ≤ `Base`; column `FirstYearAmountMinor`) goes in the in-service month on top of the method's schedule of what's left (MACRS on `Cost − FirstYearAmount`); all of `Base` needs no life. One schedule only: no separate book and tax. `ScheduledThrough(asset, date)` = prior depreciation + schedule months ending after `PriorThrough` and on or before the date.
- `FixedAsset.ClassId` and `Loan.ClassId` (optional, changeable after posting; `FixedAsset.SetClass`, `LoanService.DescribeAsync(..., classId)`) go on depreciation, disposal catch-up and interest lines.
- `DepreciationRun` (`Source = assets.depreciation`, audit `depreciation_run`, owned `DepreciationRunLines`): `DepreciationService.RunAsync(through)` posts each active asset's due amount (scheduled − prior − posted) in one entry, Dr expense / Cr accumulated per asset. Only the latest posted run can be voided, and not once one of its assets is disposed.
- Disposal (`FixedAsset.DisposalSource = assets.disposal`, `SourceId` = asset): posts depreciation due through the date (kept as `DisposalDepreciation`), Dr accumulated, Dr proceeds (Bank or Other current asset), Cr asset cost, gain/loss to "Gain or Loss on Disposal of Assets" (other income). `UndoDisposalAsync` voids it.
- `Loan.CreditLimit` (a line of credit: owned `LoanDraws`, `LoanService.DrawAsync`/`VoidDrawAsync`, `Loan.DrawSource` = `assets.loan_draw`; `Balance` = principal + draws − principal repaid) and `Loan.FactorFee` (a merchant advance, rate 0: `Split` gives each repayment fee × amount / (principal + draws + fee), the last what's left). The books check's loan tie counts draws.
- `Loan` (audit `loan`, owned `LoanPayments`): `LoanService.VoidAsync` takes back a loan entered by mistake with its proceeds entry, refused while payments stand (`VoidedAt`; it leaves lists, reports and the forecast); `DescribeAsync` changes name and notes at any time. Monthly, `Amortization.Payment` rounds up to the cent so the term repays it; optional proceeds entry (`Source = assets.loan`: Dr bank/asset, Cr the liability). Payments (`assets.loan_payment`, `SourceId` = loan) split interest = one month on the current balance, the rest principal; in date order; only the latest can be voided. Terms are locked once a payment exists. Interest defaults to "Interest Expense". Variable rates: owned `LoanRateChanges` (`Loan.SetRateChange(from, rate, payment?)`/`RemoveRateChange`, only after the latest payment, `loan.rate_after_payments`; not on fee advances); `RateOn(date)`/`PaymentOn(date)` drive `Split(amount, date)` and `NextPaymentOn(date)`; `LoanService.RemainingSchedule(loan, asOf)` uses the rate and payment on that date; form `POST /loans/{id}/rates`, `/rates/{yyyy-MM-dd}/remove`; API `PUT|DELETE /api/assets/loans/{id}/rates/{from}`.
- Reports `fixed-assets` (as of), `depreciation-schedule` (year, by quarter), `loans`. Pages `/assets` (register, run depreciation, runs), `/assets/new`, `/assets/{id}`, `/assets/{id}/edit`, `/assets/runs/{id}`, `/loans`, `/loans/{id}` (accountant to change). Form posts in `Web/Endpoints/AssetEndpoints.cs`; API under `/api/assets` (`/depreciation`, `/loans`).

## Editions and professional services (L11, slice 1)

- `Company.Edition` (`General`, `ProfessionalServices`, `ECommerce`, `Nonprofit`; stored as text, existing companies backfilled `General`), chosen at setup (`SetUpCompanyCommand.Edition`) or in Settings (`UpdateCompanySettingsCommand.Edition`, null keeps it; audited in the company snapshot). An edition only changes what's shown first: `Web/Components/Editions.cs` has each edition's menu order (`Menu`; the layout renders each path through `MenuLink`, where every label is written out for the string catalog), home page shortcuts and report groups listed first. A page left out of an edition's menu still works; Settings links the ones left out. Never make data or rules depend on the edition. `Editions.Terms` names customers per edition (Donors / Clients / Customers; `TermsAsync` for pages): the menu ("/customers" is in the nonprofit and professional services menus), the customer list and form, and the Sales page use it; other pages still say "customer".
- Rate card (`Domain/Projects/BillingRates.cs`): `BillingRate` lines (client or every client; at most one of person, role, service; optional `StartsOn`), deletable (audit `billing_rate`), managed by `BillingRateService` (no two lines for the same thing starting the same day). `BillingRates.Resolve` is the one place the order lives: for the client then each parent up its tree (a project inherits its client's rates): client + person, client + role, client + service, client alone; then every client by person, role, service; then `Worker.BillRate`, then the service's price; among lines for the same thing, the latest started by the date. Time takes its rate when entered (`TimeService` builds the client chain) and stores `TimeEntry.RateSource` ("Acme · Partner", "Entered" for a typed rate); changing the card doesn't reprice time. `BillingRateService.RaiseAsync(percent, from, wholeUnits)` adds a line from `from` for each slot's line in effect then; `TimeService.RerateUnbilledAsync(from, to?, customerId?)` reprices billable unbilled time not typed (`Entered`), not adjusted and not running (`TimeEntry.Rerate`, audited); forms `/time/rates/raise|reprice`, API `POST /api/projects/rates/raise`, `/time/reprice`. `TimeService.QuoteRateAsync` answers "which rate applies" (rate card page, `GET /api/projects/time/rate`).
- `Worker.Role` (rates by role) and `Worker.WeeklyHours` (utilization). Pages `/time/rates` (accountant to change; form posts in `ProjectEndpoints`, the "for" field is `worker:{id}`, `role:{name}` or `item:{id}`), people page fields. API `/api/projects/rates`.
- Reports in "Projects and time": `unbilled-work` (as of a date: billable time, mileage and expenses not on an invoice dated by then, per client, oldest work; detail lists each piece) and `utilization` (per person: available hours = weekly hours × weekdays / 5, hours, billable share, billable hours against available, billable value, invoiced).
- A line billed for someone else's work carries `SalesLine.OwnerCustomerId` beside `Group`, so `ProjectReports` counts its income for that project (older invoices by the group's name).
- Billing a client for its projects (slice 2): an invoice's customer can take the time, mileage and billable expenses of every sub-customer billed with it (`CustomerTree.Billed`; `TimeService/MileageService/BillableExpenseService.OpenAsync` list them too). When the work belongs to more than one of them, or to a project rather than the invoice customer, each line gets `SalesLine.Group` (the owner's display name; the invoice customer's own work first, then projects by name). `SalesLine.Group` (≤ 100, all sales line tables) is display only, copied by `ToDraft`. `SalesLineGroups.Arrange` gives the lines with a heading before and a "Total for …" subtotal after each run of one group; the invoice page and `FormService` (as `FormLineStyle.Heading`/`Subtotal`, bold in the PDF) use it.
