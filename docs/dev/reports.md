## Reporting (M5)

- Every report reads lines from `ReportLedger.LinesAsync(basis, from, to)`, never from the store directly, so all reports agree. Accrual = ledger as posted (entry basis permitting). Cash = entries whose basis allows cash, minus everything sourced from invoices, bills, customer payments and bill payments; plus, for each posted payment in range, derived lines on the payment date: the deposit/payment account for the full amount, and the paid documents' accounts allocated in proportion to their lines. `SettlementPlan` splits all of a document's settlements in turn (date, then id), each taking the difference between the cumulative shares, so the last lands every line on its figure, whatever the report's range; credit applied comes off the lines first (`Less`), since it brings no money. A new accrual document type must be added to `AccrualDocuments` and given a derivation.
- `FinancialReports`: P&L sections by account type; balance sheet with "Retained earnings" (net income before the fiscal year containing the as-of date; merged into a top-level, childless Equity account named "Retained Earnings" if there is one, else a computed row) and computed "Net income" (since its start); general ledger with opening and running balances in each account's natural sign. Rows link to the general ledger for drill-down.
- Reports that need totals rather than lines (balance sheet, trial balance, opening balances, the dashboard) use `ReportLedger.TotalsAsync(basis, splitAt, to)` (each account folded into "before" and "since" lines) or `LinesSinceAsync(basis, detailFrom, to)` (history folded, detail after): on the accrual basis without a segment filter the database adds history up (`ILedgerStore.GetAccrualTotalsAsync`); otherwise it's folded in memory. Sums are exactly those of `LinesAsync` (`LedgerFoldingTests`). Folded lines have `Source = ReportLedger.BroughtForwardSource` and no entry. Cash basis loads only the payments and refunds dated in the range, the documents they pay and everything else settling those (all of them when the range starts at the beginning).
- `TrialBalanceQuery` also runs on `ReportLedger`. Income and expense accounts show this fiscal year on the trial balance and worksheet, and open at the fiscal year in the general ledger and registers; earlier years are in retained earnings, by the balance sheet's rule (`FinancialReports.PriorYears`, `RetainedEarningsAccount`; a computed row has `Guid.Empty` as its account). `DateRanges` has the period presets (calendar month/quarter, fiscal year).
- `SearchService` searches names, numbers, references, memos and amounts (`StatementValues.TryParseAmount`), ignoring case, max 20 hits per kind, newest first. Customers, vendors, items, accounts and custom field values are matched in memory; documents and journal-made entries (manual, import, opening balance, recurring) are narrowed by the stores' `SearchAsync`/`SearchMoreAsync`/`SearchManualEntriesAsync` (estimates, sales orders, credit memos and deposits in `ISalesStore.SearchMoreAsync`, purchase orders and vendor credits in `IPurchasesStore.SearchMoreAsync`; a new kind needs a heading and link in `Search.razor` and an area in `SearchAreas`) with a `DocumentSearch` (lower-case text, amount, the matched parties' and records' ids, take 21), so a search doesn't read every document. On SQLite only ASCII letters' case is ignored there. The EF queries use `ToLower()` under a local `#pragma` (CA1304/CA1311/CA1862): the culture overloads don't translate.
- Report pages share `Components/Reports` (`ReportFilter`, `ReportQuery`, `ReportSectionRows`); they're static GET forms so every report has a shareable, printable URL.

## Reports, budgets, tax lines (V1.7)

- Every report is a `ReportDefinition` in `ReportCatalog.All` (key, inputs, group) and runs through `ReportCatalog.RunAsync(key, ReportParameters)` to a `ReportTable` (columns with a `ColumnFormat`, rows with depth/style, cells with optional `ReportLink`). The screen (`ReportTableView`), CSV (`ReportCsv`), Excel and PDF (`IReportExporter`: ClosedXML, MigraDoc) all render that table, so exports match the screen. A new report = a definition + a case in `RunAsync`; it gets the generic page `/reports/{key}`, exports and saving for free. Query parameter names are the pages' own (`preset/from/to/basis/asOf/compare/accountId/year/threshold/detail/budgetId/fromMonth/toMonth`).
- Standard lists (2026-11-03, `ListReports`): `customer-balances`, `vendor-balances` (from the agings), `customer-contacts`, `vendor-contacts`, `product-list`, `account-list` (`detail=true` = include inactive; `ReportPage.ListReportKeys` relabels the choice), `open-sales-orders`, `open-purchase-orders`, `received-not-billed` (as of a date: `PurchaseOrderService.ProgressAsync(…, asOf)` counts deliveries and bills dated by then, `PurchaseOrder.ReceivedBy`; with the account's balance then), `purchases-by-item` (bill item lines plus deliveries received into stock), `deposit-detail` (`DepositService.ContentsAsync`), `estimates-by-customer`.
- Charts on the generic report page: `ReportCharts.Pick(key, table)` (Web/Components/Reports) is opt-in by key: income against expenses per amount column (not "Total") for multi-column P&L, by class and by location; the ten largest positive rows for sales by customer / item, expenses by vendor, giving by donor. `ReportChart` draws it as SVG with the dashboard's `.chart` styles and series colors (legend only for two series, `<title>` tooltips); the table below stays the record.
- Dedicated pages (trial balance, GL, journal, aging, 1099, sales tax) keep their markup and add `<ReportActions Key=…/>` for export/save. Literal `/reports/...` routes win over `/reports/{Key}`.
- Comparisons: `Comparisons.Columns` (previous period, previous year, months, quarters; max 24 columns). Multi-column statements build each column with `FinancialReports.BuildProfitAndLoss/BuildBalanceSheet` and a shared `show` set so every column has the same rows; `StatementTables` zips them and adds `DerivedColumn`s ($ change, %, total).
- Cash flow (indirect): cash = Bank accounts; operating = net income + current assets/liabilities (incl. A/R, A/P, cards); investing = fixed/other assets; financing = long-term liabilities and equity. Net change always equals the bank balance change.
- Sales by customer and expenses by vendor read `ReportLedger` (so they tie to the P&L on either basis) and attribute lines by `Source`/`SourceId` to the document's customer/vendor. Sales by item is document-based (accrual).
- `Budget` (Domain/Reporting): monthly amounts (natural sign, P&L accounts only) for one fiscal year; owned `BudgetLines`; deletable (audit `Deleted`). `SavedReport` stores report key + query string (presets stay relative); saving under an existing name updates it. Audit types `budget`, `saved_report`.
- Tax summary table: net profit row (Schedule C "line 31"), half of 24b meals taken off, and on Schedule C the mileage log's standard mileage value under "Not in the books" (`StatementReports` reads `IProjectStore`). Schedule C has line 30 (`c.30`).
- `TaxLine.SeparatelyStated` (Schedule K items for 1065 and 1120-S, keys `p.k5`, `s.k12a`: out of `TaxSummary.Income`/`Deductions`) and `HalfDeductible` (`p.meals`, `s.meals`, `k.meals`, besides Schedule C 24b); the tax summary adds a Schedule M-1 worksheet for 1065, 1120-S and 1120. No Schedule L mapping.
- `estimated-tax` (`StatementReports.EstimatedTaxAsync(year, percent)`, `percent` query 1–60, default 25): the IRS's four periods, net profit, set-aside, payments = debits to equity accounts named "Estimated Tax" (the sole proprietor chart has Owner's Equity:Estimated Tax Payments). A worksheet, not a tax calculation.
- Tax lines: `TaxLines` catalog per `TaxForm` (from `BusinessType`), keys stored in `Account.TaxLineKey` (P&L accounts only; cleared on a change to a balance sheet type). Unmapped subaccounts use the nearest parent's line in `StatementReports.TaxSummaryAsync`. Keys are stable; relabel lines for new form years, never rekey.
- Grid forms (budget amounts, tax line mapping) and save/delete of saved reports post to `Web/Endpoints/ReportEndpoints.cs` binding `IFormCollection`.
- Reports added 2026-11-06: `budget-by-class` (`StatementReports.BudgetByClassAsync`: every budget for a class in the fiscal year of the date, side by side), `grant-budget-vs-actual` (`ReportInputs.Grant`; `grantId=all` gives every grant whose period meets the report's, a section and subtotal each), `inventory-rollforward` (`InventoryReports`), `profit-by-product` (`ActivityReports`; sales from `SoldByItemAsync` plus `ChannelOrderService.SoldAsync`, cost from movements). The three nonprofit statements take `Compare`, activities `Segments`, and a nonprofit's accountant package adds `NonprofitReports`. Budgets go in and out as CSV (`BudgetService.ToCsvAsync`, `ImportCsvAsync`; `/reports/budgets/{id}/csv|upload`).
- ClosedXML is MIT (0.105.x); text cells are written as text so nothing exports as a formula, and CSV prefixes `=`, `+`, `@` and non-numeric `-` with `'`.

## Report builder, dashboard and scheduled reports (L6, slice 3)

- `ReportBuilder` (catalog key `builder`, page `/reports/builder`, group "Custom"): ledger lines from `ReportLedger` (so it ties to the statements) with `BuilderOptions` read from the query string (`columns` comma list of `BuilderColumn`, `groupBy`, `accounts` (subaccounts included), `accountClass`, `types` (labels from `ReportCatalog.DocumentType`), `name`, `memo`, `min`/`max` on the absolute amount, `title`). Names come from each document's customer or vendor (or an expense's payee). At most 5,000 lines. Because every setting is in the query, saving, export and scheduled email work unchanged. New document sources need a label in `ReportCatalog.DocumentType` and, if they belong to a customer or vendor, a line in `ReportBuilder.NamesAsync`.
- `KpiService.SnapshotAsync(asOf)` (company default basis): cash (Bank), A/R and A/P with overdue amounts from the agings, month and fiscal-year-to-date income/expenses against the same period last year, gross margin, current and quick ratios, DSO, twelve `MonthFigures`, top five expense accounts. Catalog key `kpis`. Page `/dashboard` draws tiles and two SVG charts (series colors `--series-1/2` in `app.css`, light and dark) with a table view; hidden panels are kept per user in `DashboardPreference` (`DashboardService`, not audited), posted to `/dashboard/panels`.
- A subscription tries every recipient; whoever it didn't reach stays in `RetryRecipients` and is tried on the next runs for `RetryFor` (a day), and the home to-do list shows failed schedules. A saved query drops `asOf` when it's today, so "as of today" stays relative. PDFs stop at `MaxPdfRows` (`report.too_large_for_pdf`). The builder's totals count every line found, including those cut from the rows.
- `ReportSubscription` (audit `report_subscription`, cascade-deleted with its saved report): recipients (≤ 10), daily / weekly (day 0–6) / monthly (day 1–28) at an hour in the company's time zone (`IsDue(now, zone)`), PDF / Excel / CSV. `IsDue`: active and a slot has passed since it was created or last ran (missed slots send once). `ReportSubscriptionService.SendDueAsync` (run hourly by `RecurringPostingService`) renders through `ReportCatalog` + `IReportExporter`/`ReportCsv`, emails each recipient via `EmailService.SendMessageAsync`, records the outcome on the subscription and an `Emailed` audit record. Page `/reports/schedules` (accountant; `POST /reports/schedules/{id}` changes one in place, or with only `active` pauses/resumes it); form posts in `Web/Endpoints/DashboardEndpoints.cs`; API `/api/reports/kpis`, `/api/reports/schedules`.
