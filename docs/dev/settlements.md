# Trucking settlements

Developer notes (index in `CLAUDE.md`). Read this file before changing this area; keep it up to date in the same commit.

## Carriers and settlements (L15, A1, 2026-11-25)

- `Domain/Settlements`: `Carrier` (audit `carrier`, Versioned; name unique ignoring case, unit, deposit account (Bank or Other current asset), optional vendor, class, location and `DueAccountId`; owned `CarrierCode`s keyed by **section and code**, since one code can be a reimbursement and a deduction) and `Settlement` (audit `settlement`, Versioned; number unique per carrier except Dismissed ones, a filtered index; owned `SettlementLine`s and `SettlementDeposit`s, so it loads with `AsSplitQuery`). Waiting (changeable: `ChangeAsync` replaces every line) → Posted (`Source = settlements.settlement`) → void puts it back to Waiting; Dismissed for duplicates. Separate from `ChannelPayout`, whose lines have fixed types, one class per payout, no quantities and tax routing.
- Amounts are signed on lines: positive is money to the owner. `SettlementSections.Counted/Printed` turn a printed amount round in the sections printed positive that take money (Deduction, Wage); Other is printed with its own sign.
- The net has two parts: `NetPaid` (never negative; into `Deposits`, or the carrier's account without any) and `CarriedForward` (negative owed to the carrier, positive held by it), posted to `Carrier.DueAccountId`, "Due to Carrier" found or made when first needed. `Difference` = both less the lines; posting is refused unless it's zero and every line has an account (its own, else its code's). A posted entry needs two lines (`settlement.nothing_to_post`), so a week that nets to zero posts and one that cancels out in one account doesn't.
- Postings are one journal line per account and class, dated the pay date so the bank feed matches it. A line's class is its own, its code's or the carrier's; the deposit and carried-forward lines have none. `PostedAccountId`/`PostedClassId` keep where each line went; `SegmentService.ReclassifyAsync` moves `PostedClassId` with its journal line, so miles follow the class.
- Miles and gallons are on the lines only: never in the journal.
- Line accounts are never A/R, A/P or an item's inventory asset account (`settlement.line_account`): the books check couldn't explain those. What a settlement already has stays usable on it once deactivated.
- `SettlementService.PossibleDepositsAsync`: posted receives of the same amount in the deposit accounts within `BankFeedService.MatchWindowDays` (a bank rule may have added the deposit first). Shown, never refused.
- `SettlementGuess.Account` suggests an account from a code's words and the chart's names (the trucking accounts' and others like them); a person always chooses.
- `SettlementReader.Read(text, reference)`: pasted rows or CSV, headers in any order or `PasteOrder` without one; "0713" dates take the year that puts them within a month after the reference or before it; a file can hold several settlements by number or pay date.
- Audit snapshots list section, code, load and amount, never descriptions: those can name other people.
- Roles: entering, changing and posting are standard; carriers and their codes are accountant (`/settlements/carriers`). Own area `settlements` (`PageAreas`, `ApiScopes`, `RecordAreas`, `SearchAreas`).
- Pages `/settlements`, `/settlements/new`, `/settlements/{id}`, `/settlements/{id}/edit` (one interactive page for both, with `FormDraft`), `/settlements/carriers[/{id}]`; form posts in `SettlementEndpoints`; API `/api/settlements` and `/api/settlements/carriers`.
- Also: books check documents, the to-do list, Close the books, the cash forecast (waiting nets, and last year's), expenses by vendor and the report builder's Name (the carrier's vendor), search, erasing a vendor (an active carrier blocks it).

## Import and review (A2, A3, 2026-11-25)

- `SettlementService.ImportAsync(carrierId, bytes, date, save)`: every settlement of a CSV file waits, or none of them comes in; a number the carrier has is left out; a net below zero becomes carried forward; a file without a net leaves 0 and says so (never the lines' total, which would prove nothing). `Settlement.FileHash` warns about the same file. Form `POST /settlements/import` (one new settlement opens its page), API `POST /api/settlements/import?carrierId=&date=&dryRun=`. Fuzz target `settlement-csv`.
- `SettlementReview` (`SettlementService.ReviewAsync`, `GET /api/settlements/{id}/review`, `SettlementReviewCard`): against up to `Weeks` (3) earlier settlements of the carrier, new codes, codes on every one of them but this, and codes that came to the same amount on at least `RegularAfter` (2) of them until now; lines to a loan's liability account; section totals against the last and `StatedTotals`; account balances against `StatedBalances` (balance sheet accounts, in their own sign). A waiting settlement's own effect is added to the books' balance. Nothing is refused. The form asks again for the balances checked last time.

## Reports (A4, 2026-11-25)

- `SettlementReports` (group "Trucking"): `operating-statement` (P&L for the period, fiscal year to the period's end and all dates, each with a Per mile column built beside `StatementTables.ProfitAndLoss`; one `LinesSinceAsync` read), `settlement-loads`, `settlement-codes`, `settlement-balances` (balance sheet accounts but banks; natural sign; what's carried forward counts in the due account). The last two go in the accountant's package when used.
- Miles come from `IMileageSource`s (`SettlementMileage`: posted settlements' lines, on the pay date, with the line's posted class; another kind of trucking adds its own, and mustn't give the same miles twice). Per mile divides only lines dated since the first miles' `Covers` (the first settlement's period start), so history without miles doesn't count against them; a tag filter leaves it blank (miles have no tags); class and location filters include what's under them.

## Reading statements (A5, A6, 2026-11-25)

- `SettlementCaptureService.ReadAsync(carrier, file, pages, with, date)`: a person chooses the pages; `IStatementPages.Extract` (PDFsharp) takes only those out, and only they are sent, read and attached (`statement pages 1-4.pdf`). A PDF that can't be split is refused (`settlement.cannot_split`), never read whole. `FileHash` is of the whole file, so the same one is noticed; a taken number gets " (2)" and a note. Reading makes a Waiting settlement with `ReadMethod` (`claude`, `text`, `ocr`) and `ReadNote`; it never posts.
- Claude: `ClaudeSettlementReader` (Infrastructure) over `ClaudeRequest`, which `ClaudeDocumentReader` shares (SDK call, failures as `DocumentReadException`, file block, schema helpers). Streamed, 48,000 tokens, 8 minutes: a statement can have a hundred lines. Uses the document reading settings (key, model, the admin's switch). Printed signs turn round through `SettlementSections.Counted`, like the CSV. Every field of its schema is required and only numbers are nullable (10), text being empty when missing: Anthropic refuses more than 16 nullable (union) fields, and called 19 optional ones "too complex" (both found on the first live reads, 2026-11-25; `The_schemas_sent_to_Claude_stay_within_its_limits` checks both readers).
- On this server: `StatementPages.WordsAsync` gives a PDF's own words (PdfPig, exact) or, for pictures, Tesseract's table of words; the page's biggest image is doubled and softened first, which on a real 1-bit scan took the misread amounts from about a third of them to 1 of 54 (that one pointed out, with a few codes and words misread too). `SettlementTextParser` places words in rows, finds parts by their headings, amounts under their column's heading (the nearest right edge), miles under theirs, gallons before "GAL", and printed totals. It leaves out what it can't place; a line that starts like one but has no readable amount is kept at 0.
- **OCR is a backup and treated as wrong until checked.** `SettlementLine.Doubt` says why a line needs looking at (a word below `SureEnough`, letters put back as digits, no amount, its part not adding up to the printed total, or no total read). `Settlement.Confirm`: every doubted line ticked (or changed), all lines ticked as checked, and the net **typed** (a scan's net is never taken); `EnsureCanPost` refuses before that (`settlement.not_checked`). Changing a scan's lines keeps the doubt on lines that say the same and asks for the check again. Two mistakes that cancel raise no doubt, which is why the whole is always checked. `CheckedBy` is in the audit snapshot.
- `OpenBook:Ocr:TesseractPath` (the inbox's setting) turns it on; without it pictures are refused here (`settlement.cannot_read_here`).
- Pages `/settlements/new/statement` (interactive: file, page thumbnails, Claude first, this computer folded below with its warning), `SettlementScanCheck` and `SettlementReadBanner` on the settlement; API `POST /api/settlements/read?carrierId=&pages=1-4&with=` and `POST /{id}/confirm`. Tests use `FakeStatementPages` (words instead of Tesseract, small PDFs built by hand); fuzz targets `settlement-words`, `settlement-answer`, `settlement-pdf`.

## Load check (A7, 2026-11-25)

- `Carrier.Shares` (percent per `LoadCharge`), `CarrierCode.Charge` (which charge a code pays) and `Settlement.Billed` (load, charge, amount from the freight bills) give `ReviewLoad`s in the review: billed × share against what every settlement of the carrier, dismissed ones aside, has paid on that load's lines. Short only beyond a cent a line, which carriers lose rounding. A billed row's optional miles are compared with those of the lines that paid that charge (so surcharge lines repeating them don't count twice). While nothing known paid a charge and a revenue code on that load has no charge set, it isn't called short; the review names the codes (`Unsorted`). A code form without a `charge:i` field keeps the code's charge. Typed only: freight bills differ too much between carriers for a reader to say which of their amounts the share is of.

## Receipts on the road (2026-11-27)

- `TruckReceipt` (Domain/Settlements/TruckReceipts.cs; audit `truck_receipt`, area `settlements`; not Versioned: never changed once made) sits beside the spend it records (`BankTransactionId`, unique). The money, date, payee, vendor, account and class (the truck) are the spend's and its entry's first category line, read back each time (`TruckReceiptView`, batched: `GetTransactionsAsync`, `GetEntriesAsync`, `GetLineSegmentsAsync`), so a change, void or reclassify in Banking shows here. The receipt keeps only what a spend has no place for: `TruckReceiptKind`, `Gallons` (fuel and DEF only; required for fuel; 3 decimals, column `GallonsTenThousandths`), `Jurisdiction` (`Jurisdictions`: US states, DC and Canada's provinces and territories by postal code; a full name is taken too; required for fuel), `City`, `Number`.
- `TruckReceiptService.RecordAsync(command, fileName, photo)`: everything checked first, then in one transaction `BankingService.RecordAsync` (a spend from a bank or card account, one split with the class and a memo like "Fuel · 110 gal · Amarillo, TX"), the receipt, and the photo attached to `bank_transaction`. No account: `UsualAccountAsync` (the account of the kind's latest standing receipt, else an active expense or COGS account named as in `TruckReceiptKinds.AccountNames`). `UsualClassAsync`: the latest receipt's class, else the only active carrier's. `ReceiptId` records an uploaded `ReceiptCapture` instead (`ReceiptService.RecordedByAsync`: its files copied onto the spend, marked Recorded, in the same transaction).
- `VoidAsync` voids the spend (and audits the receipt). There's no Change: void and Copy (`/settlements/receipts/new?copy=`), or change the spend. `FuelByJurisdictionAsync(from, to)`: standing fuel receipts by state; the IFTA return's miles per state aren't kept, and the page says so.
- Pages `/settlements/receipts` (by period, default the calendar quarter; fuel by state), `/settlements/receipts/new` (static multipart form; a refusal or "Save and enter another" comes back with the fields in the query string, `SettlementEndpoints.Query`), `/settlements/receipts/{id}` (void, copy, the spend's attachments). The spend's page links back (`ForTransactionAsync`). The trucking edition's home links here instead of Purchases → Receipts. API `/api/settlements/receipts` (`GET ?from&to`, `GET fuel-by-state?from&to`, `GET {id}`, `POST`, `POST {id}/void`).

## Fixed costs by truck (2026-11-27)

- `FixedCostService.ListAsync(asOf)` / `TableAsync` (report `truck-fixed-costs`, group Trucking, `ReportInputs.AsOf`; menus Settlements and Dispatch): by class (a `Truck` with the class names its unit; no class is "Not on a truck", last). Read only; nothing is stored.
- Sources, each a `FixedCost` with what it is each time, `Month` (cost; null when it isn't one) and `CashMonth`: active templates with a next date (recurring expenses and bills: a row per template, account and class; P&L lines are costs, balance sheet lines cash only; recurring journal entries: P&L lines only, as costs, no cash); loans not voided or paid off, `NextPaymentOn(asOf)` split by `Split` (interest is the cost, the payment the cash), left out when a recurring expense or bill has a line on its liability account (the template is the payment); active depreciating assets: the schedule's month containing `asOf`, less the first-year amount in the first month, no cash; carriers' Deduction and Wage lines the same by section, code, posted account and class on the latest `SettlementReview.RegularAfter` (2) posted settlements, the latest within `CarrierStoppedAfterDays` (35), spread by the days between them. Owner-operators' deductions (B3) are income to the carrier, so aren't costs.
- Month from a schedule: weeks × 365.25 / 84, months, quarters and years divided once (`Monthly`), so 1,200 a year is exactly 100. Miles a month: every `IMileageSource`'s miles of the last `MilesDays` (91) × 365.25 / 12 / 91; per mile divides costs only. The total's per mile counts unclassed miles too.

## Owner-operators (L16, B1, 2026-11-26)

The carrier's side: the company pays owner-operators by settlement and dispatches loads. Separate records from a carrier's settlements (Part A); they share the area `settlements` (pages under `/dispatch`, API `/api/dispatch`, its `ApiScopes` area and `RecordAreas`).

- `Domain/Settlements/Owners.cs`: `OwnerOperator` (audit `owner_operator`, Versioned; one per vendor, which can't change: its bills and 1099 count under it), with `PayAccountId` (expense or COGS, "Owner-Operator Pay"), `ReceivableAccountId` (other current asset, "Owner Receivables") and `ReserveAccountId` (other current liability, "Owner Reserves"), each found or made on first use and shared by owners unless chosen; owned `OwnerReserve`s numbered from 1 (fixed, per mile or percent of earnings, an optional target and yearly interest, an `Opening` already held). A reserve is stopped, never removed. The receivable can't change while charges are open, nor the reserve account while a reserve has a balance.
- `Truck` (audit `truck`, Versioned): unit unique ignoring case, an owner or none (ours), a class for reports.
- `OwnerCharge` (audit `owner_charge`, Versioned; `Source = settlements.owner_charge`): fuel, tolls, repairs, advances; Dr the owner's receivable (class: the truck's, else the owner's), Cr the account the money came from (bank, card or other current account, never the receivable). No account = already in the books: nothing posts (`PostedHere` false). `Recovered` is what standing settlements took back; a charge with any taken back can't be voided. `Carried` charges are made only by a settlement (what a deduction couldn't take). `Counted1099` marks money that went to the owner and is already on their 1099.
- Books check `owner-receivables`: open charges that OpenBook posted, less everything recovered, against the owners' receivable accounts (accrual), from `OwnerCharge.Source` lines; anything else there is listed.
- Roles: owners and their reserves are accountant (`/dispatch/owners`); trucks and charges standard. `Company.PaysOwnerOperators` puts Dispatch in the menus (`Editions.Menu(…, dispatch)`), before Sales; it only changes what's shown.
- Audit snapshots of charges keep kind, amount and reference, never descriptions.

## The weekly run (B2, 2026-11-26)

- `OwnerSettlement` (audit `owner_settlement`, Versioned; numbered once across all owners, a unique index) with owned `OwnerSettlementLine`s (`OwnerLineKind`: Earning, Reimbursement, Withholding, Deduction, Charge, ReserveAdded, ReserveReleased, Left), each signed (positive pays the owner) with its account and class (the truck's, else the owner's), load, truck, miles, `Reportable` (earnings only), and what it points at (`ChargeId`, `ReserveNumber`, `DeductionNumber`, `LoadId` + `DispatchNumber`). `Net` is never below zero.
- `OwnerSettlementService.PreviewAsync`/`RunAsync(OwnerRunCommand)` (pattern: `CommissionService.RunAsync`): per owner, the typed pay and reserve releases, then backup withholding on the reportable gross (`BackupWithholding.On`), open charges dated up to `Through` oldest first, then reserves (they're the owner's own money, so they wait for what's owed), each only as far as what's left. All owners in one transaction; the withholding account is made before planning (making it saves).
- Net above zero: a bill through `BillService.CreateAsync` (internal overload: `ownerSettlementId`, and `approvalTotal` = gross, so approval compares the gross), reference "Settlement n", due on its date, one line per account and class (`OwnerSettlement.Postings`). Exactly zero: an entry of its own, `Source = settlements.owner`, since bills stay above zero; nothing at all to post is refused.
- `Bill.OwnerSettlementId`: `BillService.ChangeAsync` and the public `VoidAsync` refuse it (`bill.from_settlement`); `VoidFromSettlementAsync` is the settlement's. `OwnerSettlementService.VoidAsync` voids only the owner's latest standing settlement (`owner_settlement.not_latest`), unrecovers its charges, voids the charges it left, then voids its bill (paid ones refuse) or entry, in one transaction.
- Reserve balances (`OwnerService.ReserveBalancesAsync`) are the opening plus standing settlements' ReserveAdded and ReserveReleased lines (interest from B3).
- 1099 (`PayablesService.Payments1099Async`): a row per standing settlement, dated its date, entity `owner_settlement`: `ReportableGross − Net − charges taken back that were Counted1099`, with `Withheld`. The bill's payment counts the net as usual; settlement bills are left out of the not-reportable share. Withholding on payment (`BackupWithholdingService.OnBills`) skips settlement bills: the settlement held it back from the gross. The withholding report lists settlements ("Owner settlement", gross and withheld); the books check `backup-withholding` counts their withholding (bill lines of settlement bills, and `settlements.owner` lines).
- Books check `owner-reserves`: standing settlements' reserve lines against the owners' reserve accounts (accrual); settlement bills' lines and `settlements.owner` are ours, the rest listed. `owner-receivables` also counts those two sources' lines; a bill that isn't a settlement's on those accounts is someone's own.
- `SegmentService` reclassifying a settlement's bill or entry moves the settlement lines of that account and class too, so `OwnerMileage` (an `IMileageSource`: standing settlements' lines with miles, their class) follows.
- Pages `/dispatch/settlements` (list), `/dispatch/settlements/new` (interactive run with preview and `FormDraft` "owner-run"), `/dispatch/settlements/{id}` (lines, void); the bill page links to its settlement and offers no Change or Void. API `/api/dispatch/settlements` (`preview`, `run`, `{id}/void`).
## Deductions and interest (B3, 2026-11-26)

- `OwnerDeduction`s, owned by the owner (numbered, `OwnerDeductions`): fixed or per mile, an account (anything but A/R, A/P, a bank or a card: income for a charge-back, an asset for a balance paid off), `UpTo` (stops once standing settlements' deduction lines took that much: a balance or a target), `Priority`, `StartsOn`/`EndsOn`, `CarryShort`. Changes from the next settlement; stopped, never removed.
- The run takes them after backup withholding and before charges, by priority then number. Short with `CarryShort`: a Deduction line for the whole amount and a `Left` line (Dr the receivable) for the rest, and `OwnerCharge.Left` (`FromSettlementId`) made with the settlement; it can't be voided alone, and voiding the settlement voids it. Without: only what fits is taken, with a note.
- `ReserveInterestRun` (audit `reserve_interest`, Versioned; `Source = settlements.reserve_interest`, one entry dated the month's last day): Dr the expense ("Interest Paid on Owner Reserves", other expense, made on first use, or the last run's, or one chosen), Cr each owner's reserve account. One standing run a month; lines per owner reserve with the balance at month end (settlements dated to it, interest before it) × rate / 12. Interest counts in reserve balances and the `owner-reserves` check; not on the 1099-NEC.
- Page `/dispatch/interest`; API `PUT /api/dispatch/owners/{id}/deductions/{number}`, `GET|POST /api/dispatch/interest` (`due?month=`, `{id}/void`).

## Loads (B4, 2026-11-26)

- `Load` (audit `load`, Versioned; numbered from 1001, unique): customer, reference, `LoadStatus` (Booked → Dispatched → InTransit → Delivered; Cancelled), miles, commodity, weight, memo, `InvoiceId` (B5), owned `LoadStop`s (pickup or delivery, place, number, appointment window and arrived/departed, all `DateTimeOffset` in UTC; pages convert with `CompanyTime.Zone`), `LoadChargeLine`s (a `LoadCharge` kind, quantity × rate to the cent, an optional item for invoicing) and `Dispatch`es (truck once per load, driver, loaded and empty miles, share percent). Loads with all three collections read with `AsSplitQuery`.
- Status: a dispatch makes it Dispatched (and none Booked again); `RecordStop` moves it: leaving the first pickup is InTransit, leaving the last delivery Delivered, taking a time back moves it back. Times on a stop that's the same after a change stay. Cancelled and invoiced loads can't change; a dispatch paid on a standing owner settlement can't change and blocks cancelling (`load.dispatch_paid`).
- `Load.Warnings`: split shares not 100%, trucks' loaded miles not the load's, nothing charged. Shown, never refused.
- Audit snapshots give stops by kind and time, not places (they name the customer's customers).
- Pages `/dispatch/loads` (board by status; `?all=true` a table), `/dispatch/loads/new` and `/{id}/edit` (interactive, `FormDraft` "load-new"), `/dispatch/loads/{id}` (stop times by datetime-local forms in the company's time, status); API `/api/dispatch/loads` (`{id}/stops/{sequence}`, `status`, `cancel`, `restore`).

## Invoicing loads and pay rules (B5, 2026-11-26)

- `LoadService.InvoiceAsync`: a Delivered load whose charges all have items becomes one invoice through `InvoiceService.CreateAsync` (a line per charge, the reference in the memo), in one transaction with `Load.InvoiceId`. A load is billed while that invoice stands (`LoadView.Billed`): a voided one is forgotten (`ForgetVoidedInvoiceAsync`, audited) before a change, a cancel or invoicing again, so the invoice service needs no hook.
- Detention: `Load.DetentionHours(free)` sums each stop's time beyond the free hours, rounded up to the quarter hour; `ChargeDetentionAsync` replaces the Detention charge with those hours at the rate.
- `OwnerOperator.PayBasis` (Typed, PercentOfLinehaul, PerMile, Flat; backfilled "Typed"), `PayRate`, `PayEmptyMiles`, owned `OwnerPassThrough`s (percent per non-linehaul `LoadCharge`). The run (`OwnerRunEntry.Loads`, on by default) adds, before typed pay, each dispatch of the owner's trucks on a Delivered load whose last delivery was left by `Through` (or marked delivered without times) and not on a standing settlement: linehaul × share × percent, or the truck's loaded (and empty) miles × rate, or the flat rate × share; then each pass-through charge × share × percent (tolls as a Reimbursement). Lines carry `LoadId`, `DispatchNumber`, quantity and rate; a paid dispatch can't change and blocks cancelling, until its settlement is voided.

## The 1099 (checked 2026-11-26)

Checked against IRS sources instead of an accountant (the user's choice); the guide's chapter 34, The 1099, says what people need.

- Pay for hauling freight usually needs no 1099-NEC: the freight exception of Treas. Reg. § 1.6041-3, which IRS Information Letter 2001-0101 (not binding) applies to payments to independent truckers. Many carriers send one anyway, as a record that owners are contractors. Other work (unloading, installing, final mile) may need one; neither source gives a test. So nothing is counted unless the vendor's `Track1099` is ticked, which the guide and the Owners page now say only when a 1099 is sent.
- Backup withholding applies only to reportable payments (§ 3406(b)), so exempt hauling pay needs none: it stays per vendor (`WithholdsOn`), never automatic.
- When a 1099 is sent: gross before the carrier's deductions (common practice; no source speaks to carrier deductions directly), reimbursements left out only when the owner accounts for them (receipts), $2,000 threshold for 2026 (IRS instructions).
- Not found in any source: reserves counting when held back rather than when paid out, and reserve interest on a 1099-INT (Treas. Reg. § 1.6049-5 mentions escrow interest, but not who reports it). The guide tells people to ask their accountant if unsure.
