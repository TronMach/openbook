## Banking (M2)

- Bank and credit card accounts are accounts of type `Bank` / `CreditCard`. Amounts in banking code are in the account's natural sign (`BankingRules.Natural`): positive = money in the bank, or owed on a card.
- `BankTransaction` is a document (Spend / Receive / Transfer, with payee and check number) that posts one journal entry with `Source = bank.*` and `SourceId` = the document id. Categories are the entry's lines. Documents are voided, never edited (except corrections in place, below); the journal page refuses to void entries posted by documents. `BankingService.ChangeAsync` corrects a spend or receive in place (entry voided and reposted; account and kind fixed; not a cash count's over/short; transfers go through `ChangeTransferAsync` instead (accounts, amount, date, memo; feed-backed ones keep amount and accounts; page `/banking/transfers/{id}/edit`, API `PUT /api/banking/transfers/{id}`); reconciled refused by `StageVoidAsync`). Feed lines added as it or matched to its entry (`BankFeedService.TiedToAsync`) keep its amount, and matched ones follow the new entry (`BankFeedItem.FollowEntry`; sales receipt corrections do the same); an issued check (printed, or sent by positive pay) keeps amount, payee and number; billable marks point at entry line numbers (bank line 1, splits from 2), so marked splits stay in place. `SplitsAsync` reads the current splits with classes. Page `/banking/transactions/{id}/edit`, API `PUT /api/banking/transactions/{id}`.
- Corrections keep what the document already has, though it's been deactivated since: `StageVoidAsync` remembers the voided entry's accounts, classes, location and tags for the scope (so void before staging the replacement), `SalesLineBuilder.BuildCorrectionAsync` the items, and Change pages offer them (`ItemService.ListForDocumentAsync`, `SegmentOptions.LoadAsync(sp, onDocument)`).
- Services that create several entries in one save use `PostingEngine.StageAsync` / `StageVoidAsync` (internal) and save once; `NextEntryNumberAsync` counts staged entries. Mutate documents only after the staged call succeeds, so a refusal leaves nothing changed in memory.
- Reconciliation state lives in `Reconciliations` / `ReconciliationLines` referencing ledger lines; the ledger itself is never touched. One in progress per account, ticks saved as made. Voiding is blocked for any entry with a ticked or reconciled line. Voided entries and their reversals are left out of worksheets and reports.
- Statement CSV import: `BankImportService` (headers matched loosely, starting at the first of the first 20 records that has a date and an amount header (`Csv.FromHeader`), so a bank's lines above it are skipped; the delimiter is the one found most, outside quotes, on any of the first 20 lines; one signed amount column or money-out/money-in columns) with `StatementValues` for dates and amounts. It refuses to guess on ambiguous amounts ("1.005") and flags ambiguous date orders. Possible duplicates = same date, signed amount and payee as an existing posted transaction.

## Banking pro (V1.2)

- Statement files: `Ofx.Parse` (Application/Import) reads OFX/QFX/QBO, SGML or XML, with a regex tag scan (no XML parser); a file with more than one account is refused (`ofx.several_accounts`). The preview carries each line's occurrence (`BankImportRow.Occurrence`, `FeedLine.Occurrence`), so the second of two identical lines comes in alone. `BankFeedService.PreviewAsync` takes OFX or CSV; nothing posts at import.
- `BankFeedItem` is the review queue (Pending / Added / Matched / Excluded). `PostingEngine.StageVoidAsync` puts lines tied to the voided entry back to Pending (`ListFeedItemsTiedToEntryAsync`), audited; a correction that posts a replacement passes `keepFeedLines: true` and follows the match. `DedupeKey` is unique per account: `fit:<FITID>`, else a text key of date, amount, payee and reference with `#n` for the nth identical line in one file. Re-importing skips known keys. An id arriving with another amount, or a date more than `MatchWindowDays` away, is another line the bank numbered the same (`BankFeedItem.ReusedKey`: the id plus its date and amount); a date moved less is the same line. The preview also flags `PossibleDuplicate` for a line with the date and amount of one already in the feed, whatever its status.
- Add stages a `BankTransaction` through `BankingService`; Match links an existing ledger line of the same holder-view amount within 7 days; Reopen (undo) voids the transaction for an added line. The audit log links feed lines and imports through `GET /banking/feed-lines/{id}` (`BankFeedService.LocateAsync`: line id or import batch id), which opens the account's feed on the line's tab (`?show=`).
- A spend or receive can't be categorized to A/R or A/P (`bank.category_receivable`; an older line stays on a correction). A line a rule can't add waits for review and `FeedImportResult.NotAdded` says why; a rule never fits a line on its own category account. Connections move a quiet account on to a month behind today, and report an account the service no longer lists.
- A feed line added takes `AddFeedItemCommand.Splits` (adding up to the line), `ClassId`, `LocationId`; rules carry a class and location too (splits aren't on rules). `MatchAsync(itemId, entryIds)` ties one line to several entries that add up to it: `MatchedJournalEntryId` is the first, the rest are owned `MoreEntries` (`BankFeedItemEntries`); read them through `MatchedEntryIds`. `FollowEntry(from, to)`; voiding any of them sends the whole line back. `FeedReviewItem.PartMatches` are the smaller entries near it. `FeedDocumentService` (bank accounts only): `CandidatesAsync` offers open invoices (money in) or approved open bills (money out) whose balance is the line's amount, dated by it; `ApplyAsync(item, kind, id)` records `PaymentService.ReceiveAsync` / `BillPaymentService.PayAsync` on the line's date and reference and matches the line, in one `IUnitOfWork` transaction. API `GET /api/banking/accounts/{id}/feed/documents`, `POST /api/banking/feed/{id}/pay-document`.
- `BankRule.Splits` (owned `BankRuleSplits`: account, percent, class; 2 to `MaxSplits`, adding to 100; `BankRuleService.SetSplitsAsync`, form `/banking/rules/{id}/splits` with `sacct:i/spct:i/sclass:i`): `SplitsFor(amount)` (by `Shares.ByWeight`) goes on auto-added lines' `AddFeedItemCommand.Splits` and on `FeedSuggestion.Splits`, which pre-fills the review row's split.
- `BankRule`: first active rule in `Priority` order whose text is in payee or memo (plus direction, amount range, account) wins. AutoAdd rules post at import, except a line with a possible match (same amount within `MatchWindowDays`), which waits for review so it can't double; others only suggest. A vendor only goes on money-out rules.
- `BankingRules.IsReconcilable` (bank, card, other current asset, other asset) gates reconciliation, so an investment account reconciles to a broker's statement (register link). The statement of financial position splits out board-designated net assets (equity accounts named "Board-designated…", `NonprofitReports.IsBoardDesignated`); the nonprofit template has Investments, Investment Return, Board-Designated and Endowment Net Assets.
- `ReconciliationService.StartAsync` ticks the lines feed items stand for (added from or matched to, dated by the statement, not reconciled before). `ReconciliationService.UndoAsync` reopens only the latest completed reconciliation of an account, and only when none is in progress; ticks are kept.
- Checks: `CheckService` lists bank (not card) spends, bill payments, customer refunds and retainer refunds (`CheckKind.CustomerRefund`/`RetainerRefund`; both carry `PrintedAt`, `PositivePaySentAt`, `PositivePayVoidSentAt` like bill payments; `PrintableCheck.OwnerId` is the retainer; positive pay lists and marks them, and `ListSpendReferencesAsync` counts their references for the next check number), not payments in an ACH batch nor spends from statement or feed lines (their money has left; printing an ACH one is refused, `check.sent_by_ach`, and the page ticks only those with a check number); printing (`PrintAsync(checks, firstNumber)`) numbers checks that have none from `firstNumber` in the order given (digits, keeping zeros; `AssignCheckNumber` on spends, bill payments and both refunds) and renders `CheckForm`s (voucher layout, absolutely positioned MigraDoc frames, `AmountInWords.ForCheck`) and sets `PrintedAt` with an audit record. Printing goes through the web form endpoint `/checks/print` (antiforgery), which binds `IFormCollection`: minimal APIs on .NET 10 can't bind a `string[]` form parameter.
- Blazor `[SupplyParameterFromQuery]` can't bind enums: take a string and `Enum.TryParse`.
- Audit entity types: bank_rule, bank_feed_item, bank_feed_import.

## Banking advanced (L4, slice 1)

- Live bank feeds: `BankConnection` (Domain/Banking; owned `BankConnectionAccount`s keyed by the service's account id) holds the access credential protected by `ISecretProtector` (excluded from the data export and audit snapshots). `IBankConnectionClient` per provider; only SimpleFIN so far (`SimpleFin` parser in Application, `SimpleFinClient` HTTP in Infrastructure: setup token = Base64 claim URL, POST once for the access URL, GET `/accounts` with Basic auth, fetched in 60-day pieces, https only). `BankConnectionService.SyncAsync` fetches posted lines from `Start` (last fetched date − 10 days, else `ImportFrom`, else 30 days back) into `BankFeedService.QueueAsync` with `FitId` = the service's transaction id, so overlaps dedupe; pending lines wait. Failures are kept on the connection (`LastSyncFailed`, `LastError`), not thrown. `BankSyncHostedService` syncs due connections (every 6 h) as "system". Pages `/banking/connections` (accountant). Tests use `FakeSimpleFinBridge` behind the real client.
- Positive pay: `PositivePayService` lists numbered checks (digits-only references) on a Bank account, spends (not those from statement or feed lines, `ImportBatchId`: their money has left, and the reference is the bank's; check number suggestions skip them too) and bill payments, voids marked `V`; the first file for an account goes back `FirstFileDays` (90); the CSV is ASCII (account number, check number, date, amount, payee, void). Each check records what the bank knows (`PositivePaySentAt`, `PositivePayVoidSentAt`, on spends and bill payments): with no dates the file is `UnsentAsync`, new checks and voids of sent ones whatever their date; dates send a range again. A sent check keeps its amount, payee and number. `PositivePaySettings` per account remembers the bank account number, date format, header and how far exports went. Page `/banking/positive-pay`; audit type `positive_pay_export`.
- Petty cash: `PettyCashFund` (a Bank account with a `FloatAmount`) and `CashCount`s. `PettyCashService.CountAsync` stages an over/short spend or receive against "Cash Over and Short" (created on first use) in the same save; `ReplenishAsync` transfers float − balance. Spending from the box is an ordinary spend. Pages `/banking/petty-cash`, `/banking/petty-cash/{id}`; audit type `petty_cash_fund`. Form posts for all three live in `Web/Endpoints/BankingEndpoints.cs`; API under `/api/banking/connections`, `/positive-pay`, `/petty-cash`.

- Recurring expenses (2026-11-03): `RecurringExpense` (Domain/Banking; audit `recurring_expense`, Versioned; owned `RecurringExpenseLines` as `BankSplit`s; paid from a Bank or CreditCard account, optional vendor and payee, `Recurrence` schedule, location and tags). `RecurringExpenseService.CreateDueAsync` records each occurrence through `BankingService.RecordAsync` (a spend), saving each on its own and stopping a template at its first error; the hourly job's "Recurring expenses" step runs automatic ones. Pages `/banking/recurring`, `/banking/recurring/new|{id}`; API `/api/banking/recurring-expenses` (+ `/{id}/active`, `/record-due`).
- Fuzzing (2026-11-12): `StatementValues.TryParseAmount` refuses anything beyond `Money.MaxAmount` (sums of a channel report's lines overflowed `decimal`); `SimpleFin.Date` treats a time no date can hold as unreadable (`FromUnixTimeSeconds` threw, failing the sync). Tests in `Fuzz/FuzzFindingsTests`.
- Petty cash routine (2026-11-13): the box's page links **Record spending** to `/banking/{account}/record/spend?back=/banking/petty-cash/{id}?done=spent`; `BankTransactionNew` takes `back` (through `AccessRules.LocalPath`) for where to go once saved and on Cancel, and the box then says the spending was recorded. Tests: `RecordPageAccessTests`.
