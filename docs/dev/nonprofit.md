## Nonprofit (L11 nonprofit, slices 1–3)

- Donor restrictions live on accounts and classes, not in a new dimension: `Account.DonorRestricted` (income, other income and equity only, `Account.CanBeRestricted`; cleared by a type change to anything else; changing it on an account with lines in a closed period needs the closing password, since it restates those years; `CreateAccountCommand`/`UpdateAccountCommand.DonorRestricted`, null keeps it; chart CSVs take a "Donor restricted" column) and the purpose is the line's class. Net assets "with donor restrictions" = every line on a restricted account (equity and income, all time); "without" = the rest of equity and the P&L.
- `RestrictionRelease` (Domain/Nonprofit, `Source = nonprofit.release`, audit `restriction_release`): `NonprofitService.ReleaseAsync` posts Dr "Released From Restricted" (restricted) / Cr "Released To Unrestricted", children of the income account "Net Assets Released from Restrictions" (`ReleaseAccountsAsync`, created on first use; the nonprofit starter chart has them), both on the purpose's class; refused beyond what the purpose holds on the date. Void reverses. `PurposesAsync(asOf)` (accrual) gives per class: received, released, balance, spent (expense lines on that class or its subclasses since its first restricted gift, each going to the nearest class up the tree that holds restricted money) and the suggested release (spent − released, up to the balance; none for lines without a class).
- `Segment.FunctionalArea` (classes only: Program / ManagementAndGeneral / Fundraising; null takes the parent's, `FunctionalAreas.Deciding`). `SetUpFunctionalClassesAsync` adds "Programs", "Management and General", "Fundraising" and turns class tracking on. In the functional expense statement each class directly under the class that sets program services is a program column (deeper classes roll up into it).
- `NonprofitReports` (on `ReportLedger`; group "Nonprofit"): `statement-of-activities` (P&L built per column with relabeled totals, plus net assets at both ends), `statement-of-financial-position` (balance sheet with the equity section replaced by the two net asset rows), `functional-expenses` (`StatementTables.Expenses`), `restricted-net-assets`.
- Gifts and pledges (slice 2, `Domain/Nonprofit/Gifts.cs`, `GiftService`): donors are customers. `Donation` (`Source = nonprofit.donation`, audit `donation`, own number sequence): money into a Bank account or Undeposited Funds (other current asset; Make deposit picks it up through `GiftDeposits`, an `IUndepositedSource`) or `GiftKind.InKind` to an asset or expense account (description required; the letter describes it without a value); income defaults to "Contributions" / "In-kind Contributions" (created on first use); `GoodsValue`/`GoodsDescription` for quid pro quo (`Deductible`). `Donation.ExchangeAccountId` (optional, unrestricted income; not on pledge payments; `RecordGiftCommand`/`ChangeGiftCommand.ExchangeAccountId`, form `exchangeAccountId`) takes `ExchangePart` (the goods value, up to the gift) off the contribution line; release checks count only the contributed part. `Pledge` (`Source = nonprofit.pledge`, audit `pledge`) posts Dr "Pledges Receivable" / Cr income with `EntryBasis.AccrualOnly`; a gift naming it posts Dr bank / Cr the receivable (Both) plus a `CashOnly` entry Dr receivable / Cr income (`Donation.CashEntryId`), so cash basis counts pledged money when paid and the bank has one line; voiding the gift voids both. `Pledge.Installments` (owned `PledgeInstallments`, posting nothing; `SetInstallments` adds up to the amount, `Pledge.Spread`, `DueBy`, `NextDueAfter`; `GiftService.SetInstallmentsAsync`, audited `Updated`; form `/nonprofit/pledges/{id}/installments` with `count`/`months`/`first` or `clear`) feed the open pledges report's Next due and Overdue columns. `GiftService.PledgeStatementAsync(id, asOf)` is the donor's statement (a `DonorLetter`, `GET /nonprofit/pledges/{id}/statement`). A pledge can't be voided once paid on; `CancelRestAsync` writes off the unpaid part instead (`Pledge.CancelSource`, accrual only, Dr income / Cr receivable on its class; `UndoCancelAsync` voids it), after which no more payments are taken. Paid amounts are summed from posted gifts (`GiftService.Paid`). `RecurringGift` (audit `recurring_gift`, `RecurringGiftService`, page `/nonprofit/recurring`, API `/api/nonprofit/recurring-gifts`): a donor, amount, bank or holding account, optional income account, class and pledge, on the shared `Recurrence` schedule; `CreateDueAsync` records each occurrence through `GiftService.RecordAsync` (reference "Recurring"), saving each on its own and stopping a template at its first error; the hourly `RecurringPostingService` runs automatic ones.
- `GiftService.ChangeAsync(id, ChangeGiftCommand)` corrects a gift in place (entry voided and reposted, same id and number, kind fixed; pledge payments refused, `donation.change_pledge`; amount and account fixed while deposited, in a payout or feed-matched, and the match follows; `EnsureNotReleasedAsync` for what leaves a restricted purpose). Page `/nonprofit/gifts/{id}/edit` (version-checked), post the same path.
- Donors: `CenterService` lists gifts, pledges and grants; `SearchKind.Gift/Pledge/Grant` (area nonprofit; `INonprofitStore.SearchAsync`); `GiftService.GivingAsync` feeds the Giving card on the customer page; `/nonprofit/gifts?donor=`. `RecordBatchAsync` (`GiftBatchCommand`: shared date and `GiftBatchDefaults`, rows, control total; one transaction; page `/nonprofit/gifts/batch`). `GiftImportService` (preview, all or nothing, new donors added, a standing gift with the same donor, date, amount and reference answers for one incoming; page `/nonprofit/gifts/import`, API `POST /api/nonprofit/gifts/import?accountId=&dryRun=`).
- `AllocationRule` (Domain/Nonprofit/Allocations.cs; audit `allocation_rule`, owned `AllocationShares` adding to 100%): one expense account, from a pool (a class, or none), spread over classes. `AllocationService.RunAsync` posts one entry per rule (`AllocationRun`, `Source = nonprofit.allocation`, audit `allocation_run`, Cr pool / Dr classes, split by `Shares.ByWeight`), and since the credit lands in the pool a second run moves only what's new. Accrual lines only, and not on document forms or recurring templates. Page `/nonprofit/allocations`, API `/api/nonprofit/allocations`.
- Online gifts: the gift import reads processors' exports (gross, charge id), and `ChannelService.RecordedGiftsAsync` clears a payout's charge with a gift's reference (any channel, into the gift's holding account), setting `Donation.PayoutId`, which leaves the deposit list and blocks voiding until the payout is voided. No hosted donation page.
- Letters: `DonorLetter` (Application/Forms) rendered by `IFormRenderer.Render(IReadOnlyList<DonorLetter>)` (a section per letter, pages numbered per letter). `GiftService.AcknowledgmentAsync` / `YearStatementAsync` / `YearStatementsAsync` (all donors, one PDF); wording: per-gift quid pro quo sentence or "No goods or services were provided…", in-kind note, and the 501(c)(3) sentence with the EIN from `GiftSettings` (one row, audit `gift_settings`). Emailing goes through `EmailService.SendMessageAsync`; an emailed acknowledgment marks the gift `AcknowledgedAt` (audit `Emailed`); statements are audited `Emailed` on the customer. `NonprofitReports.NetAssetRollForwardAsync` (`net-asset-rollforward`), `GiftReports.InKindGiftsAsync` (`in-kind-gifts`); `GiftReports`: `schedule-b-contributors` (year, $5,000 or more, money and in kind), `giving-by-donor`, `giving-by-campaign` (period), `lapsed-donors` (fiscal year starting in `year`, default this one), `open-pledges` (as of). `Donation.Campaign` (free text ≤ 100, beside the books: `SetCampaignAsync` any time, audited; `CampaignsAsync` suggests; `RecordGiftCommand.Campaign`, `GiftBatchDefaults.Campaign`, the import's Campaign/Appeal column; `PUT /api/nonprofit/gifts/{id}/campaign`).
- Pages `/nonprofit/gifts`, `/nonprofit/gifts/new` (`?pledge=`, `?donor=`), `/nonprofit/gifts/{id}` (letter PDF at `/nonprofit/gifts/{id}/letter`), `/nonprofit/pledges`, `/nonprofit/pledges/new`, `/nonprofit/pledges/{id}`, `/nonprofit/thanks` (unthanked gifts, statements by year: `/nonprofit/statements/{year}` and `/{year}/{donorId}` PDFs), `/nonprofit/settings` (accountant). Gifts, pledges, thanks and statements need the standard role to change; the rest of `/nonprofit` the accountant role. API `/api/nonprofit/gifts`, `/pledges`, `/statements/{year}/{donorId}`, `/settings`.
- Grants on the cash basis: `Grant.CashOnReceipt` (new grants): the award and recognitions post `AccrualOnly`, and each receipt adds a `CashOnly` entry (`GrantReceipt.CashEntryId`, Dr receivable and advance / Cr income), voided with it, so cash-basis income comes with the money. Grants entered before keep their posted entries.
- Grants (slice 3, `Domain/Nonprofit/Grants.cs`, `GrantService`, audit `grant`): funder = customer; `ClassId` is the grant (null in `GrantCommand` creates a class named after it: under `ParentClassId` when given, taking that program's area, else top-level with `FunctionalArea.Program`); `form-990-expenses` leaves cost of goods sold out of line 25 and shows it apart; spending = expense lines on that class or its subclasses within `StartsOn`–`EndsOn` (accrual), by account against owned `GrantBudgetLines` (`GrantPosition`; a subaccount without a budget line of its own counts against its nearest budgeted parent). Unconditional: `AwardEntryId` (`Source = nonprofit.grant`: Dr "Grants Receivable" / Cr income, both on the class). Conditional: nothing at award; `RecognizeAsync(through)` posts min(spent, award) − recognized (`nonprofit.grant_recognition`). Receipts (`nonprofit.grant_receipt`: Dr bank, or a holding account such as Undeposited Funds, / Cr receivable; `GrantDeposits` is the `IUndepositedSource`, `DepositedKind.Grant` keyed by the receipt's `EntryId`, `GrantReceipt.DepositId` set by the deposit and refusing a void, `grant.receipt_deposited`) are capped at the award; on a conditional grant the part beyond the receivable (`Grant.AdvancePartOf`) credits "Refundable Advances" (other current liability, `AdvanceAccountId`, created on first use) and recognitions debit that advance first (`AdvanceUsedBy`); each row keeps its `AdvancePart`. A receipt whose advance has been earned since, or a recognition the funder has paid since, can't be voided. Receipts and recognitions are owned rows with their entry, voided individually; `SourceId` is the grant for all three sources. Receivable = an existing "Grants Receivable" (other current asset or A/R), else a new other current asset; income defaults to "Grants". `grants` and `form-990-expenses` reports in `NonprofitReports` (Part IX from each expense account's Form 990 tax line, nearest parent's when unmapped, by functional area). Chart CSVs take a "Tax line" column (keys like `n.9.7`); the nonprofit template maps its accounts.
- `Grant.FundingSharePercent` (conditional grants, default 100, column `FundingShareTenThousandths`; `SetFundingShareAsync`, form `/nonprofit/grants/{id}/share`, API `PUT /api/nonprofit/grants/{id}/share`): `GrantPosition.ReadyToRecognize` counts `FunderShareOf(Spent)`; `Match` is the rest. No indirect rate, effort reporting or SEFA yet.
- `GrantAdjustment` (owned, table `GrantAdjustments`, `Grant.AdjustmentSource` = `nonprofit.grant_adjustment`): `ReduceAsync` (up to `Grant.Reducible`: the receivable on an unconditional grant, posting Dr income / Cr receivable, accrual only with `CashOnReceipt`; what isn't recognized on a conditional one, posting nothing) and `ReturnAdvanceAsync` (up to `Advance`: Dr refundable advance / Cr bank, plus a cash-only Dr income / Cr advance with `CashOnReceipt`); `AmendAsync` adds an `Increase` row (unconditional: Dr receivable / Cr income); voiding one is refused once payments or income count on it (`grant.increase_used`). `VoidAdjustmentAsync`. `NetAward` = Amount − reductions + increases caps receipts and recognitions; `Advance` and `Due` count returns. Form posts `/nonprofit/grants/{id}/reduce|return-advance|adjustments/{line}/void`, same under `/api/nonprofit/grants`.
- `GrantReportDue` (owned, table `GrantReportsDue`: due date, title, `SentOn`; posts nothing): `AddReportDueAsync`, `MarkReportSentAsync`, `RemoveReportDueAsync` (form posts `/nonprofit/grants/{id}/reports[/{line}/sent|remove]`); `RemindersAsync(today)` feeds the to-do list (reports due within 14 days or late, grants ending within 30 days, spending ready to recognize). No email reminders.
- `GrantRequest` (owned, table `GrantRequests`): a reimbursement request, posting nothing, paid by a receipt (`ReceiveAsync(..., requestLine)`; voiding the payment frees it). `GrantService.RequestAsync`, `VoidRequestAsync`, `RequestLetterAsync` (a `DonorLetter`). Posts under `/nonprofit/grants/{id}/requests`; API `POST /api/nonprofit/grants/{id}/requests`.
- Pages `/nonprofit/grants`, `/nonprofit/grants/new`, `/nonprofit/grants/{id}` (budget grid posts `account:{i}`/`amount:{i}`, receive, recognize, void rows with `VoidLine`, close/reopen/void); accountant role to change. API `/api/nonprofit/grants` (`/{id}/budget`, `/receipts`, `/recognize`, `/close`, `/void`).
- Pages `/nonprofit` (purposes with a release form per row, releases, classes and their areas) and `/nonprofit/releases/{id}`; a class's area on its segment page (posts `/segments/{id}/functional-area`); the account form shows the restricted box in the nonprofit edition (or when already set). Form posts in `Web/Endpoints/NonprofitEndpoints.cs`; `/nonprofit` needs the accountant role to change. API `/api/nonprofit` (`/purposes`, `/releases`, `/functional-classes`; scope area `nonprofit`) and `PUT /api/segments/{id}/functional-area`.
