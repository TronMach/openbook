# Sales tax

Developer notes moved out of `CLAUDE.md` (index there). Read this file before changing this area; keep it up to date in the same commit.

## Sales tax (V1.5)

- Domain in `Domain/SalesTax`: `TaxAgency` (owns a liability account: a subaccount of "Sales Tax Payable", created by `SalesTaxService` on agency creation), `TaxRate` (one agency, percent to 4 decimals, stored as ten-thousandths), `TaxCode` (1–10 rates in print order; "combined" when more than one), `SalesTaxPayment` (`Source = salestax.payment`).
- Tax lines are `SalesLine`s of kind `Tax`, appended after the other lines by `SalesLineBuilder.BuildAsync(drafts, customer, taxCodeId, date)`: one per rate of the code, even when zero, with `TaxRateId`, `Rate` = the percent charged and `IncomeAccountId` = the agency's liability account, so `SalesLine.Postings` credits it and cash-basis allocation covers it. They're never drafted: use `SalesLine.Drafts(lines)` to copy a document's lines (`ToDraft()` throws on a tax line).
- Dated rates: `TaxRate.Changes` (owned `TaxRateChanges`, key rate + `EffectiveFrom`; at most `MaxChanges`; `SetChange`/`RemoveChange`, `SalesTaxService.SetRateChangeAsync`, form `/sales-tax/rates/{id}/changes`, API `PUT|DELETE /api/sales-tax/rates/{id}/changes/{from}`); `PercentOn(date)` is the latest change by then, else `Percent` (the rate before the first change). `SalesTaxService.ComponentsAsync(…, on)` and `ListChoicesAsync(on)` take the date (null: today), and the forms reload their choices when the document's date changes; `SalesLineBuilder.BuildAsync` passes the document's. `Customer.ExemptionStartsOn` (`CustomerDetails.ExemptionStartsOn`) bounds `ExemptionOn(date)` from below.
- `TaxCalculator` is the one place for the math (the web preview calls it too): taxable amount = taxable item lines less each discount's taxable share; tax = taxable × combined percent rounded once, split by rate through `Shares.ByWeight` (the running total, so a 0% rate gets nothing and none goes negative).
- Corrections keep what was sold: `BuildCorrectionAsync` keeps each continuing line's income account (unless it's inactive), list rate and price source (`SalesLine.KeepFrom`, by `FromLines`, else by position). A sub-customer billed with its parent is covered by the parent's exemption (`CustomerTree.ExemptionOn`, `ExemptionHolder`).
- Typed tax (C328, 2026-11-15): `TaxAmount` on the create and change commands of invoices, receipts and credit memos (`SalesLineBuilder.BuildAsync(…, typedTax, ct)`) replaces the worked-out total, split between the charged (not exempt) rates by percent (`TaxCalculator.Split(…, typed)`). It must be in cents, between zero and the taxable amount (`tax.typed_out_of_range`), with a rate to go on (`tax.typed_no_rate`) and a code (`tax.typed_without_code`). Nothing is stored about it: `TaxCalculator.TypedTax(lines, exemption)` sees a typed amount as tax lines that differ from what their rates give. A correction keeps it while the taxable amount is unchanged (else works it out), unless it types another or sets `WorkOutTax`. The forms show **Tax amount** under the tax lines (`SalesLinesEditor.TypedTax`, `@bind-TaxAmount`; the edit pages start it at `TypedTax`, and blank sends `WorkOutTax`); it's not kept in form drafts. Estimates and sales orders don't take one.
- Exemptions by state (C326, 2026-11-15): `Customer.ExemptionRegions` ("TX, OK", `NormalizeRegions`; null = every state, cleared with the certificate) limits the certificate to agencies with those `Region`s. `TaxComponent.Region` carries the agency's; `Customer.ExemptFrom(component)` decides, so an agency without a state isn't covered by a certificate that names states. `TaxCalculator.Split`/`TaxLines` take a `Func<TaxComponent, bool>` (the bool overloads call it), and the exempt rates leave the combined rate, so the rest round on their own. `TaxExemption` text adds ", for TX". Corrections: `TaxCalculator.KeptExemption(lines, exemption)`: every rate when nothing was taxed, else the rates whose stored tax line was zero. Forms pass `SalesLinesEditor.ExemptFrom`; `FormService.TaxTotals` prints the charged lines and then the exemption; the liability report counts a rate's taxable amount as exempt only where its line is zero on an exempt document.
- Corrections keep the tax charged: `SalesLineBuilder.BuildCorrectionAsync` with `TaxAsCharged`, used by the three `Change…` services: under the same customer and code the tax is recomputed on the new lines at the stored rates (`TaxCalculator.Charged`) and stored exemption, even if the code can't be charged now; the Change pages list the document's code at those rates (`ListChoicesForDocumentAsync`). A code with active customers can't be made inactive (`tax.code_in_use`); `SalesTaxField.CustomerCodeOff` warns on forms for a customer already on one.
- Command `TaxCodeId`: null takes the customer's code, `Guid.Empty` charges no tax. Documents store `TaxCodeId` and `TaxExemption` (the customer's exemption text when valid on the document date; tax lines are then zero). Recurring invoices use the customer's code; estimates pass theirs to the invoice.
- Line `Taxable` defaults from the item (`Item.Taxable`; existing items were backfilled taxable); `SalesLineDraft.Taxable` null means "the item's setting".
- Inactive codes, rates and agencies can't be charged (`tax.code_inactive`, …); a rate in an active code and an agency with active rates can't be deactivated.
- `TaxAgency.Region` (one agency per state): `ChannelPayout.Postings(channel, taxAccounts)` sends a Tax or MarketplaceTax line with that ship-to `Region` to the agency's account, and `LiabilityAsync` adds an "Online sales" row (`TaxRateId` empty) from posted payouts. Lines with no region stay on the channel's tax account. Already posted payouts aren't moved.
- Economic nexus: `NexusThreshold` (Domain/SalesTax; table `NexusThresholds`, one per `Region`, sales and optional transactions; audit `nexus_threshold`, deletable) kept by hand at `/sales-tax/nexus` (`NexusEndpoints`); `NexusService.SalesByStateAsync(from, to)` (invoices and receipts less credit memos by the customer's shipping else billing state via `AddressParts`, channel payout lines by `Region`) behind report `sales-by-state`; `UnregisteredAsync(today)` (past the threshold over twelve months, no active agency with that `Region`) on the to-do list. API `/api/sales-tax/nexus`, `/sales-by-state`.
- `SalesTaxReports.LiabilityAsync(from, to, basis)` works from the documents (accrual by default; cash counts each invoice by the share paid in the period through payments on their date, less those returned on their return date, and retainers applied, the share of what applied credits left (the credits' own lines taken off, as `SettlementPlan.Less` does); invoices at zero on their date, receipts as they are, credit memos by refunds, and `OwedAsync(asOf, basis)` reads the cash-basis ledger; the report's `basis` input): invoices and receipts plus credit memos negated, each counted on every rate of its code, one row per rate and percent charged; `SalesTaxPaymentService.OwedForPeriodAsync` is the balance at a period's end less payments dated after it for that period; owed figures are the liability accounts' ledger balances. `FilingPeriods.LastCompleted` gives the period to file. `TaxAgency.DueDays` (1–120, default 20, `TaxAgencyDetails.DueDays` null keeps it; `DueOn(periodEnd)`) and `SalesTaxPaymentService.ReturnsDueAsync(today)` (active agencies owing for their last whole period, not recorded as filed: `SalesTaxFiling`, Domain/SalesTax, table `SalesTaxFilings` unique per agency and period end, posts nothing, audit `sales_tax_filing`; `RecordFilingAsync`/`RemoveFilingAsync`/`FilingsAsync`; hub "Mark filed" posts `/sales-tax/filings`, `/sales-tax/filings/{id}/remove`; API `GET|POST /api/sales-tax/filings`, `DELETE /{id}`) feed the to-do line "Sales tax returns to file".
- `SalesFigures` (2026-11-24): what counts as sales on the liability report, by state and by item leaves out untaxed item lines on other current liability or asset accounts (gift cards, retainage).
- Use tax (2026-11-24): `UseTax` (Domain/SalesTax; table `UseTaxes`, unique per `DocumentId`, owned `UseTaxLines`, one per rate at the percent charged) on a bill, spend or vendor credit: `UseTaxInput(TaxCodeId, Amount?)` on their create and change commands (null on a change keeps it, `Guid.Empty` takes it off). `UseTaxService.PlanAsync` works it out with `TaxCalculator.Split` on the lines whose account can carry it (`UseTax.CanCarry`: expense accounts and fixed assets), never item lines, so stock, landed costs and billable mark-ups leave it out; `UseTax.Spread` gives the debits (largest remainder, per account and class). It posts last in the document's own entry (`UseTax.FirstLine`, `Covers(entry, line)`), A/P or the bank unchanged; the document's `Total`/`Amount` leaves it out. Anything that reads a spend's entry lines as its categories must skip what `Covers` says (`BankingService.SplitsAsync`, `BillableExpenseService`, the 1099 shares). Corrections under the same code keep the rates charged, and a typed amount while the taxable amount is unchanged. Not on recurring templates, copies, bank feed adds or bills from purchase orders. The document's audit snapshot carries it (`UseTaxService.Snapshot`).
- Use tax counts on the document's date on both bases, because it's owed when something is bought: `ReportLedger` keeps a bill's or vendor credit's use tax lines (both sides) as posted on the cash basis, while the bill's own expense waits for its payment, so the cash-basis agency balance, the liability report (`TaxLiabilityRow.IsUseTax`, one row per agency from `ListUseTaxesAsync`) and tax payments agree. Books check `use-tax`: each document's kept lines are what its entry owes each agency. `RateChargedAsync` counts use tax. API `GET /api/sales-tax/use-tax/{documentId}`.
- `FormService.TaxTotals` gives the subtotal/tax rows for screens and PDFs; tax lines never show as ordinary lines.
- Audit entity types: tax_agency, tax_rate, tax_code, sales_tax_payment (attachments allowed on sales_tax_payment and now shown on customers, for certificates).
