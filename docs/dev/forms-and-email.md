# Printed forms, printing and email

Developer notes moved out of `CLAUDE.md` (index there). Read this file before changing this area; keep it up to date in the same commit.

## Form designs

- `FormDesign` (Domain/Companies/FormDesign.cs; audit `form_style`, page `/settings/forms/{id}`): a saved look for printed documents: template, accent color, footer, `FontKey`, `PaperSize` (Letter/A4), logo (PNG or JPEG ≤ 1 MB recognized by its bytes, stored on the row, left out of the data export; position left/right/center, size small/medium/large), `Show` (`FormParts` flags stored as a number: company address/phone/email, customer contact, ship-to, terms, page numbers, savings row, totals, acceptance lines on estimates, striped rows; `ShowShipTo`/`ShowTerms`/`ShowQuantityAndRate`/`ColumnLabels` are derived, for the API's older fields), layout (`Landscape`, `FormMargins`, `FormTextSize`), the line table's columns (owned `FormDesignColumns`, see below), and per-document `FormDesignText`s (owned `FormDesignTexts`: title and message per `FormDocuments` value: invoice, estimate, credit memo, sales receipt, statement, purchase order). One `IsDefault` (can't be switched off); `DefaultFor` flags give a design some documents (`SetDefaultForAsync` takes them from whichever design had them); `Customer.FormDesignId` gives a customer theirs. `FormDesigns.Pick`: the customer's (if active), else the document's, else the default. Switched off, never deleted. Setup creates "Standard"; the migration turned the old company style columns into it.
- Columns (Domain/Companies/FormColumns.cs): `FormColumns` keys `number`, `item`, `description`, `sku` (Item.Barcode), `lots`, `class`, `taxable`, `date`, `person` (from `SalesLine.ServiceDate`/`Person`, set by `ProjectBilling` on one-entry time lines and trips; `FormService.Work` adds them as cells and `ShortDescription`, the description without its "date · person" start, which the renderer prints when either column is shown), `quantity`, `rate`, `amount` and `field:{id}` (item custom fields only: they're the ones with values per line; `formdesign.field_unknown` otherwise); 1–10 per design, unique, label ≤ 30, width 1–100 (relative weights). `FormColumn.Position` orders them (`Columns` sorts; EF loads owned rows unordered, so never read `_columns` for order). `FormColumn.AutoWidth` ("Fit": as wide as its heading and longest entry, measured by the renderer with PDFsharp's `XGraphics` measure context; `FormColumnWidths.Share` in Application gives auto columns their need (at most 60% while others share, the text column narrowed first when too wide) and the rest by weight) and `Align` (`ColumnAlign`, now in Domain; null = usual: built-in numeric right, custom fields by type via `FormDesignService.UsualAlign`; a built-in's usual value is stored as null; `FormTableColumn.AlignChosen` stops `FieldColumn` overriding a chosen one). Optional columns (all but description, number, quantity, rate, amount) drop out of a document where every line is blank. Lines carry `FormLine.Cells` by key (`FormService.Cells`: item name, barcode, lots, class full name, "Yes"/"No" taxable on taxed documents); lots no longer go into the description: the renderer appends them when there's no lots column. `CustomFieldService.DecorateAsync` runs after `Apply`: placed field columns get the field's name and numeric alignment (`FormDesignService.FieldColumn`) and print even when the field isn't marked to print; unplaced printed fields with `Placement = Column` get a column after the description when `AutoFieldColumns`, else go under the description. `FormStyle.Columns` holds the resolved `FormTableColumn`s. The migration backfilled existing designs (description/qty/rate/amount or description/amount with their headings; ship-to and terms into `Show`).
- Company font: `FormDesign.CompanyFontKey` (null = the design's font, `FormDesign.BuiltInFont` "built-in" = OpenBook Sans, else a font key; checked like `FontKey`; a font used either way can't be removed) prints the company's name and contact lines in the header (`FormStyle.CompanyFont`/`CompanyFontBuiltIn`, renderer `Look.CompanyFamily`).
- `FormDesignPresets` (Domain/Companies/FormDesignPresets.cs, stable keys): service, product, professional, bold, minimal, detailed, wide, packing-slip, ink-saver, ink-saver-compact (Minimal, black, no stripes, light fonts, fitted number columns); `CreateFromPresetAsync(key, name?)` (numbered name when taken). Gallery card on `/settings/forms` (preview `GET /settings/forms/preview?preset=`, "Use this design" posts `/settings/forms/new` with `copyFrom=preset:{key}`); API `/api/settings/form-designs/presets` (GET, `/{key}/preview`, POST `/{key}`).
- Print with another design: `FormService.RenderAsync/BuildAsync(kind, id, designId)` and `PurchaseOrderForms.RenderAsync(id, designId)` through `FormDesignService.ChosenAsync` (active designs only); `?design=` on `/api/sales/{kind}/{id}/pdf`, `/api/purchases/orders/{id}/pdf` and `/purchases/orders/{id}/pdf`; `PrintButtons` shows "Print with…" when there's more than one active design.
- `FormDesignService`: CRUD, logo, default, documents, customer choice (audited on the customer), `ForAsync(document, customerId)`, `ChosenAsync`, `DefaultStyleAsync` (donor letters), `Apply` (title keeping "(void)", message after the memo, `FormParts` applied: company and customer contact details, ship-to, terms facts, totals, the `FormService.SavingsLabel` row, `SalesForm.Acceptance` on estimates; `FormStyle.From(design)`), `PreviewAsync` (sample document with the company's details and sample cells for every column; unsaved settings, logo from a saved design, presets). Form builders make forms with `FormService.Unstyled` and a ship-to from the customer's shipping address, then apply the design (FormService for sales forms and statements, `PurchaseOrderForms`).
- Renderer (`MigraDocFormRenderer`): templates Classic, Modern, Compact, Bold, Minimal, Boxed, Elegant (header, party boxes, heading row, body rows and totals vary per template); `Look` carries the `FormStyle`, accent tint, sizes (text size) and margin; landscape swaps the page; page numbers only with `FormParts.PageNumbers`; paper size per section; logos as `base64:` MigraDoc images sized by height, narrowed to fit (`ImageSize` reads PNG/JPEG dimensions); bill-to and ship-to side by side (`Parties`); the line table prints `FormStyle.TableColumns` (notes, headings and details in the description column, else item, else the first); statements print the design's title and message. `FormParts.AccountBalance` (off by default): invoices carry `FormService.PreviousBalanceLabel`/`TotalOwedLabel`/`RetainerHeldLabel` rows (the billed customer's other open invoices dated earlier, by date, creation then number; retainers held on the date) and `Apply` drops them without it.
- Web: `/settings/forms` (list, new from a copy), `/settings/forms/{id}` (editor with a QuickBooks-Desktop-style column table posting `columnKey` per row and `col:{key}`/`order:{key}`/`label:{key}`/`width:{key}`/`auto:{key}`/`align:{key}`, `companyFontKey`, `show` per part, `margins`, `textSize`, `landscape`, `autoFieldColumns`; live preview: `wwwroot/forms.js` posts the form to `POST /settings/forms/preview` as the fields change and shows the PDF from a blob in the `[data-preview-pane]`, double-buffered (a second iframe loads it and swaps in on load, so it never goes blank), opened with `#navpanes=0&view=FitH|Fit` (Page width / Whole page, `localStorage` `ob-preview-fit`), "Larger preview" (`.design-editor.larger`, `ob-preview-larger`) and full screen (`.design-preview.full`, Esc); a refused preview shows its message in the status line and keeps the last page; the editor's sections are `<details class="design-section">` with a sticky `.design-save` bar, the page `.wide.design-page` (1680px); `GET /settings/forms/preview?design=` for the saved one), logo upload (multipart), default, documents, switch off; the customer page's "Form design" card posts to `/customers/{id}/form-design`. Form posts in `Web/Endpoints/FormDesignEndpoints.cs`; API `/api/settings/form-designs` (+ `/logo` raw body, `/default`, `/documents`, `/preview`) and `PUT /api/customers/{id}/form-design`; the old `PUT /api/settings/forms` changes the default design's template, color and footer.
- Fonts (`FormFontService`, audit `form_font`): a design's `FontKey` is null (the built-in "OpenBook Sans", from `SystemFontResolver`), a shipped font's key, or an added font's (`added-` + family). Shipped fonts are 12 Google Fonts families (SIL Open Font License, each folder with Regular.ttf, Bold.ttf and OFL.txt; `fonts.json` lists them) in `Infrastructure/Forms/Fonts`, copied beside the app and read by `BundledFonts`. Added fonts are `FormFont` rows (regular and optional bold TrueType bytes, left out of the data export): from Google Fonts by family name (`GoogleFontsClient`: the CSS2 API asked by a non-browser user agent answers with static TTF instances per weight, even for variable families; files only from https fonts.gstatic.com) or uploaded. `FormFont.Check` refuses WOFF, CFF ("OTTO") OpenType and collections. An added font can be removed only while no design uses it; a design can't name a font that doesn't exist (`font.unknown`), and a font gone missing prints with the built-in one. `FormDesignService.StyleAsync`/`ApplyAsync` load the faces into `FormStyle.Font`; the renderer registers them in `FormFontRegistry` under "OB {key} {hash}" (PDFsharp caches typefaces per process, so changed files get a new name) and `SystemFontResolver` resolves those first (bold simulated when there's no bold face). Web: the designs page's Fonts card (Google, upload, remove: `/settings/forms/fonts/google|upload|{key}/remove`), the editor's font list; API `/api/settings/form-fonts` (GET, `/google`, `/upload` with Base64 files, DELETE). Tests use `FakeGoogleFonts` behind the real client.

## Printing

- `FormScripts.Warning(form)` (Application/Forms) flags right-to-left text and letters beyond Latin Extended-B in a sales form (it doesn't read the font); `PrintButtons` builds the form and shows it beside **Print**.
- Print buttons (`<PrintButtons Document=… Id=… PdfUrl=…/>` on invoice, estimate, credit memo, sales receipt and purchase order pages; a plain `data-print` button on statements): `wwwroot/forms.js` loads the PDF into a hidden frame and calls its `print()`, opening the print dialog without leaving the page (a browser that refuses gets the PDF in a tab). `form[data-print-form]` does the same with the PDF its POST returns, then reloads the page; buttons with their own `formaction` submit normally.
- `PrintQueueItem` (Domain/Companies, table `PrintQueue`): "print later" for `PrintQueueItem.Queueable` documents (not statements), waiting until a batch prints it (`PrintedAt`/`PrintedBy`); put back from "printed recently" (30 days). A convenience list beside the books, not audited (like dashboard preferences).
- `PrintService`: queue (add once, remove, put back), `DocumentsAsync(kind, from, to)` to pick from, `PrintAsync(documents, StatementRun?)`: each document rendered as usual (its own design and page numbers; duplicates once), statements after them by customer name, joined by `IFormRenderer.Combine` (PDFsharp import), at most 200; waiting queue items in the batch are marked printed.
- Page `/print` (queue with ticks, choose documents by kind and dates, statements for customers with a balance, printed recently); linked from Sales and Purchases. Form posts in `Web/Endpoints/PrintEndpoints.cs` (`/print/queue`, `/print/queue/remove`, `/print/put-back`, `/print/pdf` with `doc` = "Document:id" in order, `customer`, `from`, `to`); API `/api/print` (`/queue`, `/printed`, `/queue/{id}/put-back`, `/pdf`), scope area `data`.

## Email (2026-11-03)

- Domain in `Domain/Email/EmailTemplates.cs`: `EmailKind` (invoice, estimate, credit memo, sales receipt, statement, purchase order, gift acknowledgment, giving statement, reminder, report, invitation; `EmailTemplates.Key` is the kebab key in addresses and the API), placeholders per kind (`EmailTemplates.Placeholders`, with samples), `Usual` wording, `Fill` (a line whose placeholders all fill blank is left out; unknown ones stay as written), `Check`/`Unknown` (templates refuse placeholders their kind doesn't take, `email_template.placeholder_unknown`; `CollectionSettings.UpdateReminders` checks too). `EmailTemplate` (table `EmailTemplates`, key = kind; none = the usual wording; reminders stay in `CollectionSettings`; audit `email_template` on the company, Created/Updated/Deleted on reset). `EmailAddresses` parses comma/semicolon lists (≤ 10). `SentEmail` (append-only history: kind, entity type and id as the audit log names them, to/cc/bcc, subject, body as written, attachment name, link, design). `DocumentLink` (`obd_` token in the email only, SHA-256 hash stored and left out of the export; invoices, estimates, credit memos, receipts; revoked, never deleted; first/last opened, at most once a minute). `Customer.EmailCc` (≤ 5) and `EmailPdf` (Attach / Link / Both), `FormDesign.EmailStyle` (Branded / Simple / Plain, in the design's audit snapshot), `ScheduledEmail.Cc/Bcc/DesignId`, `EmailSettings.LinkAddress` (where customers reach OpenBook; else the online payments' public address; without either, link emails attach the PDF and the preview says so).
- After delivery nothing may conflict: `LastEmailedAt` is written straight in the database (`ISalesStore.Mark…EmailedAsync`, past the `Version`), so the save holds only `SentEmail` and the audit record. `EmailCompose` redirects after sending (`?sent=`), so a refresh can't send again. Voided invoices, credit memos and receipts refuse (`document.voided`) and print with "(void)", cancelled purchase orders "(cancelled)". An invitation's stored body says "(link removed)".
- `EmailService` writes, previews and sends every document email through an `IEmailSource` per family (`EmailSources.cs`: `SalesEmailSource` (reminders are invoices), `PurchaseOrderEmailSource`, `GiftEmailSource`): context (values, recipient, customer, design document, pay-online, refusal), render (with an optional design), pay link, mark sent. `PurchaseOrderForms` and `GiftService` no longer send email (no cycle: the sources depend on them). `DraftAsync(EmailSource)` fills the template and the customer's Cc and link choice; `PreviewAsync` never throws on a bad address (notes instead) and never calls Stripe (buttons get "#"); `SendAsync` checks recipients, then settings, renders the PDF unless link-only, makes the link, sends, and only then adds the link, `SentEmail` and audit (the source may give its own audit, e.g. the gift's `MarkAcknowledgedAsync`) in one save, so a failed send keeps nothing. `SendTestAsync` goes to the user's email (else the from address) as "[Test] …", recording nothing. `SendEachAsync` (statements, giving statements), `SendWordedAsync` (reports, invitations: default design, history kept, caller saves), `HistoryAsync`, `RevokeLinkAsync` (audited on the document), `OpenLinkAsync` (the anonymous view), `SetCustomerPreferencesAsync` (audited on the customer). The older `DraftAsync(FormKind…)`/`SendFormAsync`/statement methods wrap the new ones.
- Payment receipts (2026-11-03): `EmailKind.PaymentReceipt` (key `payment-receipt`, appended last) through `SalesEmailSource` (placeholders amount, date, paid_by, applied, credit, balance); `EmailContext.Attaches` false means no PDF and no link (`CanLink` false; `RenderAsync` throws for it). Compose at `/sales/email/payments/{id}` ("Email receipt" on the payment page, `EmailHistory` there); API kind `payments`.
- `EmailFormatter` (Application, Markdig 0.38.0, BSD-2): the body is Markdown with raw HTML disabled, soft line breaks as breaks, autolinks; links other than http(s)/mailto become text, images become links (nothing loads); styles inline; `Layout` is a 600px table in the design's look (accent band, logo as `cid:logo` inline image; previews use a data URI and `<base target="_blank">`), buttons, footer with the company's contact lines and the design's footer. The text part drops formatting and writes links as "text (url)" and buttons as "Label: url". Plain designs send text only. `EmailMessage` gained `Cc`, `Bcc`, `HtmlBody`, `InlineImages` (init); `To` may hold several addresses joined by ", " (MailKit splits them).
- Web: `EmailCompose.razor` (Components/Pages/Emails) serves `/sales/email/{kind}/{id}` (invoices, estimates, credit-memos, receipts, statement with `from`/`to`), `/purchases/orders/{id}/email`, `/nonprofit/gifts/{id}/email`, `/nonprofit/statements/{year}/{id}/email` (an EditForm, so a refusal keeps the edits); `EmailTemplatesPage`/`EmailTemplateEdit` at `/settings/email-templates[/{key}]` (accountant). Shared components in Components/Emails: `EmailPreviewPane` (mail window: headers, sandboxed `srcdoc` frame double-buffered, Attachment tab loading the PDF with `design=`), `EmailBodyField` (toolbar and, for templates, placeholder chips), `EmailHistory` (beside Notes on invoice, estimate, credit memo, receipt, PO, gift and customer pages), `CustomerEmailCard`. `wwwroot/email.js` (delegated: posts `form[data-email-preview]` to its URL 450 ms after a change, `data-email-subject/body` rename fields, formatting with Ctrl+B/I/K, fill-ins at the cursor, test send by fetch). Endpoints in `Web/Endpoints/EmailEndpoints.cs`: `{compose path}/preview|test` (JSON; fields `Input.X` or plain names), `/settings/email-templates/{key}/preview`, `/sales/collections/settings/preview` (reminders, same editor on the collections page), `/sales/email-links/{id}/revoke`, `/customers/{id}/email-settings`, and anonymous `GET /view/{token}` (rate-limited with the links, `AccessRules` lets anyone through). The design editor's "Email look" section posts `emailStyle`. Help chapter 29.
- API (`Api/EmailApi.cs`, `EmailRequest` with to/subject/body/cc/bcc/designId/pdf over the usual draft): `POST /api/sales/{kind}/{id}/email[/preview]`, `GET …/emails`, `GET /api/customers/{id}/emails`, `PUT /api/customers/{id}/email-settings`, `POST /api/sales/email-links/{id}/revoke`, `POST /api/purchases/orders/{id}/email[/preview]`, `GET …/emails`, `POST /api/nonprofit/gifts/{id}/email`, `POST /api/nonprofit/statements/{year}/{donorId}/email`, `/api/settings/email-templates` (GET, GET/PUT/DELETE `/{key}`, POST `/{key}/preview`). Scheduled emails take `Cc`, `Bcc`, `DesignId`. Tests: `EmailTests`, `EmailTemplateTests`.
