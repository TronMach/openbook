# Finding your way around

## The top bar

Every page has the same bar across the top:

- **The menu**: Home, Accounts, Banking, Sales, Purchases, Inventory, Projects, Journal, Reports and Dashboard (the order depends on your edition, below). **Settings** appears for accountants and administrators, **Backup** for administrators.
- **Simple view** switches you to the simple view: a menu bar by task and a home page that walks through each step (see [The simple view](28-simple-view.md)). In the simple view, **Switch to full view** at the right of the menu bar comes back.
- **Search** finds customers, vendors, products, accounts and transactions.
- **Your username** opens your password and sign-in sessions.
- **The region code** (for example `en-US`) opens language and region.
- **Theme** switches between automatic, light and dark.
- **Sign out.**

Each area's main page is a hub: **Sales** lists recent invoices, payments and receipts with buttons for the common jobs (New invoice, Receive payment, Sales receipt, Credit memo, New estimate) and links to everything else in sales. **Purchases** and **Banking** work the same way.

## Editions

The edition you chose at setup decides what comes first:

| Edition | Menu leads with | Customers are called |
| --- | --- | --- |
| General | Accounts, Banking, Sales, Purchases, Inventory, Projects | Customers |
| Professional services | Projects, Time, Clients | Clients |
| E-commerce | Sales, Inventory, Purchases | Customers |
| Nonprofit | Nonprofit, Donors | Donors |

The professional services, e-commerce and nonprofit editions also put shortcuts to their most-used pages on the home page, and list their report groups first on the Reports page.

An edition never hides anything: a page left out of your edition's menu still works, and Settings links to every area. Change the edition in **Settings** at any time.

## Search

Type in the search box and press Enter. Search looks through names, document numbers, references, memos and amounts, so you can find an invoice by its number, a check by the amount written on it ("1,250.00" or "1250"), or a customer by part of their name. It shows up to 20 results of each kind.

## Choosing from long lists

Wherever you pick a customer, vendor, product or account, the field is a search box: type any part of the name, use the arrow keys to move through the matches and Enter to pick one. Every word you type has to match, in any order, ignoring capitals and accents, so `office sup` finds "Supplies:Office". Accounts can also be found by number. Esc or Tab leaves the list.

Products can be found by barcode too: scan into the product field and press Enter.

## Keyboard shortcuts

Press `?` anywhere (outside a text field) to see the list. The main ones:

| Keys | Does |
| --- | --- |
| `/` | Jump to search |
| `Ctrl` + `Enter` | Save the form you're typing in |
| `Alt` + `Q` | Jump to the quick-entry row in an account register |
| `g` then a letter | **Go to**: `h` home, `s` sales, `p` purchases, `b` banking, `i` inventory, `j` journal, `r` reports, `a` accounts, `c` customers, `v` vendors |
| `n` then a letter | **New**: `i` invoice, `r` sales receipt, `p` payment, `e` estimate, `b` bill, `j` journal entry, `c` customer, `v` vendor, `t` product or service |

Shortcuts are ignored while you're typing in a field, so they never get in the way of data entry.

## Dark mode

Select **Theme** in the top bar to cycle through *auto* (follow your computer's setting), *light* and *dark*. The choice is remembered on that browser.

## Language and region

Select the region code in the top bar. **Region** sets how dates and numbers are shown; **language** sets the text. Until you choose, OpenBook follows your browser's language. Parts of OpenBook are translated so far; anything not yet translated shows in English.

## Record pages

Most pages for a single record (a customer, an invoice, a bill) share the same pieces at the bottom:

- **Attachments**: add files such as a receipt photo, a signed contract or a tax-exemption certificate. They're stored inside the company file, so backups include them.
- **Notes**: short free-text notes, each with who wrote it and when.
- **History** (for accountants and administrators): every change to the record, who made it and what changed.

## Installing it as an app

In browsers that support it (Chrome, Edge, Safari on iOS), you can install OpenBook from the address bar or the share menu. It then opens in its own window with its own icon. It still needs to reach the OpenBook server to show your books.
