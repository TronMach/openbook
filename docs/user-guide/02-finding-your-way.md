# Finding your way around

## The top bar

Every page has the same bar across the top:

- **The menu**: Home, Accounts, Banking, Sales, Purchases, Inventory, Projects, Journal, Reports and Dashboard (the order depends on your edition, below), then **Settings**.
- **Search** finds customers, vendors, products, accounts and transactions.
- **Help** opens the chapter of this guide about the page you're on (see [Help inside OpenBook](#help-inside-openbook)).
- **Your username** opens a menu of your own things: who you're signed in as and your role, **Password and sessions**, **Two-factor sign-in**, **Offline capture on your phone**, **Language and region**, **Theme** (automatic, light or dark), **Switch to simple view** (a menu bar by task and a home page that walks through each step; see [The simple view](28-simple-view.md)), **Show the menu as drop-downs**, and **Sign out**.

### The menu as a bar or as drop-downs

The menu starts as a bar of areas: select one to go to its main page. If you'd rather reach pages directly, select your username, then **Show the menu as drop-downs**: each area becomes a drop-down listing its pages (for example **Sales** (**Clients** or **Donors** in those editions) lists customers, products and services, new invoice, receive a payment, sales orders, collections, pricing, sales tax and the rest), so most pages are one click away. Open a drop-down by selecting it; while one is open, pointing at another opens that one; Esc or a click elsewhere closes it. **Show the menu as a bar** goes back. The choice is yours alone and is remembered wherever you sign in.

### Area pages

Each area's main page is a hub: **Sales** lists recent invoices, payments and receipts, with the most common jobs (**New invoice**, **Receive payment**) beside the title and everything else in labelled groups under it: **Create** (estimate, sales receipt, credit memo), **Lists** (customers, products and services, sales orders, retainers, recurring invoices…), **Tools** (collections, sales channels, the document inbox, printing) and **Set up** (pricing, sales tax). **Purchases**, **Banking**, **Inventory**, **Accounts**, **Projects** and **Nonprofit** work the same way.

### Buttons you can't use

When your role or permission set doesn't allow something, its button or link still shows, greyed out with a dashed border, and pointing at it says what it needs ("Needs the standard role. Ask an admin."). Selecting it opens a page that says what you have, what it needs and which administrators can give it to you. Forms you can't send are greyed out the same way. Once an administrator gives you the access, they work again; you may be asked to sign in once more.

## The home page

In the full view the home page lists what's waiting under **To do**, each line linking to where it's done: overdue invoices, payments to deposit, bills due within a week, bills waiting for approval, bank lines to review, accounts to reconcile (not reconciled through the end of last month), estimates waiting for an answer, purchase orders still to receive, items to reorder, documents to check, receipts to record, figures in the books that don't agree and, for nonprofits, gifts not yet thanked. Only what you can act on is listed.

Under it, **Getting started** checks off the first steps as you do them: setting up the chart of accounts, adding bank accounts, a customer and your products and services, the first invoice, email, reconciling and scheduled backups. It goes away once every step is done. **Company** at the bottom shows the company's legal structure, currency and fiscal year.

The simple view has its own home page; see [The simple view](28-simple-view.md#the-home-page).

## Settings

**Settings** in the top bar opens a list of everything you can set up, grouped by what it's for: **Company** (company details, closing date, custom fields, your language), **Sales and getting paid** (form designs, email, online card payments, sales tax, pricing, late fees and reminders), **Purchases and stock**, **Banking**, **Accounting**, **People and security**, **Your data** (backups, moving in, imports, the audit log) and **Connections** (document reading, API keys, webhooks, add-ins, system). Each line says what the setting does and how it's set now, for example "Sending through smtp.gmail.com" or "Every day at 12:00 · last 2 Nov 2026"; lines in red are worth a look ("Off: nothing can be emailed yet", "No automatic backups"). Type in **Find a setting** to narrow the list. Everyone can open the list; settings you can't change are greyed out and say whom to ask.

**Company details** holds the company's name and contact details, legal structure, fiscal year, time zone, report basis, account numbers, the menu (edition) and view new people start with, and whether you sell more stock than is on hand.

## Help inside OpenBook

This guide is part of OpenBook. **Help** in the top bar opens the chapter about the page you're on; some pages have their own **Help** link to the right section. The guide's pages list every chapter down the side, link to each other, and have a search box that finds every heading and paragraph containing all the words you type. Press `g` then `?` to open the chapter for the current page, or `?` for the list of keyboard shortcuts, which links to the guide too.

## Editions

The edition you chose at setup decides what comes first:

| Edition | Menu leads with | Customers are called |
| --- | --- | --- |
| General | Accounts, Banking, Sales, Purchases, Inventory, Projects | Customers |
| Professional services | Projects, Time, Clients | Clients |
| E-commerce | Sales, Inventory, Purchases | Customers |
| Nonprofit | Nonprofit, Donors | Donors |

The professional services, e-commerce and nonprofit editions also put shortcuts to their most-used pages on the home page, and list their report groups first on the Reports page.

An edition never hides anything: a page left out of your edition's menu still works, and Settings links to every area. Change it in **Settings → Company details** under **Which menu suits your work?** at any time.

## Search

Type in the search box and press Enter. Search looks through names, document numbers, references, memos and amounts, so you can find an invoice by its number, a check by the amount written on it ("1,250.00" or "1250"), or a customer by part of their name. Capital letters don't matter. It shows up to 20 results of each kind, newest first; type more of the number or name to narrow it.

## Choosing from long lists

Wherever you pick a customer, vendor, product or account, the field is a search box: type any part of the name, use the arrow keys to move through the matches and Enter to pick one. Every word you type has to match, in any order, ignoring capitals and accents, so `office sup` finds "Supplies:Office". Accounts can also be found by number. Esc or Tab leaves the list.

Products can be found by barcode too: scan into the product field and press Enter.

## Keyboard shortcuts

Press `?` anywhere (outside a text field) to see the list. The main ones:

| Keys | Does |
| --- | --- |
| `/` | Jump to search |
| `Ctrl` + `Enter` | Save the form you're typing in |
| `Alt` + `Q` | Jump to the quick-entry row in an account register |
| `g` then `?` | Open this guide at the chapter for the page you're on |
| `g` then a letter | **Go to**: `h` home, `s` sales, `p` purchases, `b` banking, `i` inventory, `j` journal, `r` reports, `a` accounts, `c` customers, `v` vendors |
| `n` then a letter | **New**: `i` invoice, `r` sales receipt, `p` payment, `e` estimate, `b` bill, `j` journal entry, `c` customer, `v` vendor, `t` product or service |

Shortcuts are ignored while you're typing in a field, so they never get in the way of data entry.

## Dark mode

Select your username, then **Theme**, to cycle through *auto* (follow your computer's setting), *light* and *dark*. The choice is remembered on that browser.

## Language and region

Select your username, then **Language and region**. **Region** sets how dates and numbers are shown; **language** sets the text. Until you choose, OpenBook follows your browser's language. OpenBook is in English so far, so the language setting only matters once a translation is added; regions already work.

## Record pages

Most pages for a single record (a customer, an invoice, a bill) share the same pieces; **History** is a button at the top, the others are at the bottom:

- **Attachments**: add files such as a receipt photo, a signed contract or a tax-exemption certificate. They're stored inside the company file, so backups include them.
- **Notes**: short free-text notes, each with who wrote it and when.
- **History** (for accountants and administrators): every change to the record, who made it and what changed.

Documents you enter often have **Copy** at the top (invoices, estimates, sales receipts, sales orders, bills, purchase orders, checks and expenses, journal entries): it opens a new one filled in from this one, dated today.

## Installing it as an app

In browsers that support it (Chrome, Edge, Safari on iOS), you can install OpenBook from the address bar or the share menu. It then opens in its own window with its own icon. It still needs to reach the OpenBook server to show your books.
